# MTG ScriptForge (all websites)

MTG supplied the ScriptForge prompt on 7 November 2026 ("2D Niche Prompt for Claude"). This is that prompt adapted to
his niche, his brand and his production tools, for every one of his websites. Always apply it together with
**mtg-creative-standard** (voice, photo, music, visuals, preview) and **mtg-hooks-standard** (hooks, curiosity loop,
CTAs, tribe growth). When a website section needs a short explainer, **mtg-site-learning-system** decides which videos
that site needs; this skill writes and produces them. MTG's latest instruction in the chat always wins.

**MTG's photo is always shown in a circle** with a gold gradient ring and a soft gold glow (his rule since
7 November 2026). The whole photo fills the circle so his face, the t-shirt print and the sign stay visible; never a
zoomed face-only crop. This replaces the rounded square in older copies of mtg-creative-standard section 10a.

## This is MTG's business

MTG, 7 November 2026: "This is not a hobby. This is the serious business to me." Every video must be fun, helpful,
educational and thought provoking, AND do a job for the business: grow traffic to his websites, sign people up, close
deals honestly, make sales, grow his team of business partners, grow the MTG TRIBE and his platforms. So every video
gets:

- **A job:** one primary goal (reach, engagement, traffic, leads, sign-ups and sales, team growth) and the viewer
  journey stage it serves (discover, learn, trust, decide, grow).
- **An interaction layer:** questions that make people think and answer, a guess or a number to comment, a takeaway,
  a specific person to share it with, a reason to like, save and follow.
- **The right website address at the right moment:** the one page that fits the topic, said and shown when the viewer
  has just received value, plus the tribe.
- **The right length** for its job and platform.
- **A posting pack** so it goes out with titles, descriptions, pinned comment and share messages ready.
- **Research first** (MTG, 7 November 2026: "Don't make a video for the sake of making a video"): find the similar
  videos that get enormous traffic on each platform, learn what makes them win, and make MTG's version clearly better.
  No script without a research brief (`references/research.md`).

- **Platform-safe, always** (MTG, 7 November 2026: "I don't want my videos banned or removed, or my channels shut
  down"): every video passes the compliance gate in `references/compliance.md` before it is published. Social
  platforms get education, trust and the tribe; compensation plans, recruitment, sign-up links into those programs,
  returns and paid draws stay on MTG's websites and in the WhatsApp tribe.

The full method is in `references/engagement-and-conversion.md`, `references/research.md` and
`references/compliance.md`; read them before Step 2 and Step 3. Honesty still comes first: no income promises, no fake
urgency, no manipulation, risks shown plainly.

## Step 0: know the website

Find out which website the videos are for (the project you are in, or ask). Before suggesting ideas, read what that
site really offers: its `llms.txt`, home page, dashboard and guide pages in the repository, or the live site. Note its
products, opportunities, tools and calculators, sign-up and referral links exactly as the live pages use them, and what
is finished or still being built. `references/destinations.md` lists what is known for each site; add what you find.

## The idea: bridge, then destination

Broad, interesting topics bring in new viewers who would never search for a product name. Each of those videos is
angled toward money, crypto, safety or digital life, so its natural next step is MTG's world. Direct videos then teach
his own niche and send people to the exact page that helps them.

- **Bridge videos** (menu 1 to 5): story first, value first. One honest sentence near the end connects the topic to a
  destination. Main CTA: join the MTG TRIBE (mtgtribe.com or mtgtribe.com/group). Never bolt an income pitch onto a
  story video.
- **Direct videos** (menu 6 to 11): explainers, tutorials, honest reviews, tool trainings, myth busters, site tours.
  CTA: the one page that answers the viewer's next question, then the tribe.

Guide MTG through the steps below. Never skip a step, and wait for his choice before moving on. If he already gave the
niche or the idea, start at the step that follows.

## Step 2: 10 ideas

Before choosing ideas, run quick demand research (`references/research.md` section 1): what in this sub niche already
pulls big numbers on YouTube (`scripts/yt_research.py`), TikTok and Instagram, which formats are hot right now, and
where the gaps are. Ideas come from evidence, not guesses.

Give exactly 10 ideas for the chosen sub niche. For each:

- A curiosity driven title, under 60 characters (mtg-hooks-standard; no hype on money).
- A one line hook or angle: why someone would click.
- **Destination**: the page or platform it leads to, and why that is the honest next step.
- **Build**: GrowKaz stickman 2D (stories, What If, history, psychology), 3D studio (objects, money flows, numbers,
  products, intros), screen recording (tutorials, tool trainings) or a mix; 16:9, 9:16 or both.
- **Job**: primary goal, journey stage and length (playbook sections 1 to 3).
- **Engage**: the main comment prompt (a question, a guess, a number, a vote) and who to share it with.
- **Proof**: the evidence of demand (for example "top 3 videos on this: 2 to 10 million views; the format is hot this
  year") and the gap MTG's version fills.
- **Risk tier**: green, amber or red (`references/compliance.md` section 2). Red ideas are planned for the websites and
  the WhatsApp tribe, not for social platforms.

Mix formats: listicles, single deep dives, "the truth about...", countdowns, myth busters, story driven pieces, honest
checks. Avoid generic or overdone titles. Then ask MTG to pick a number, or ask for 10 more.

For the chosen idea, do the full research in `references/research.md`: 5 to 10 benchmark videos across platforms (3 to

## Step 3: the full script

Fit the length to the video's job (playbook section 3). For a 3 to 4 minute What If or bridge video, write a
narration script of 450 to 600 words for the main body (about 150 words per minute), plus MTG's greeting and
signature outro. Other jobs scale the same structure up (explainers, trainings) or down (Shorts: hook, one idea,
one prompt, one call to action, short sign-off).

**Structure** (with the interaction layer and address placement built in)

1. HOOK (0:00 to 0:12): a surprising fact, question or scene that stops the scroll. No greeting before it.
2. GREETING (0:12 to 0:20): "Hi, my name is MTG, Your Digital Architect. Thank you for taking the time to watch this
   training." Then one line on what the video teaches.
3. SETUP (to about 0:45): why this matters to the viewer; open a curiosity loop. `[ENGAGE]` a prediction prompt
   ("comment your guess") that the video pays off later.
4. MAIN BODY (to about 3:00): 3 to 5 escalating beats. Each ends with a mini cliffhanger or a re-hook into the next.
   `[ENGAGE]` one reflection moment in the middle ("pause, what would you have done?").
5. PAYOFF (to about 3:20): the biggest reveal or the most useful insight. It must pay off the hook and the
   prediction. `[ENGAGE]` the takeaway or vote prompt.
6. BRIDGE (one or two sentences): the honest link from the topic to the destination. `[CTA]` the primary action with
   its one main address, said and shown (playbook section 5).
7. OUTRO: `[ENGAGE]` share with a specific person, then the tribe call to action that fits the platform, then MTG's
   signature outro word for word (see mtg-creative-standard).

**Narration style by niche** (always MTG's own approved voice, warm and patient; the niche changes pace, music mood and
visuals, never the voice)

- Self-improvement: calm and deep, second person, short lines, deliberate pauses, builds to an empowering close.
- Psychology: intriguing and authoritative, everyday examples ("Have you ever noticed..."), real studies named
  accurately.
- What If: cinematic and escalating, "Imagine you wake up and...", a timeline (seconds, days, years), real science
  first, then the extreme.
- Science: wonder filled and clear, vivid everyday analogies (buses, queues, shopping, stokvels), no hype beyond the
  evidence.
- History: a storyteller opening in the middle of a dramatic moment, real names, dates and places.
- MTG's niche and the website (6 to 11): a trusted family member sitting beside the viewer; one action or idea at a
  time; exact on-screen labels for tutorials; risks and fees said plainly.

**Format**

- Section headers with timestamps.
- `[NARRATION]` text written to be spoken aloud.
- `[VISUAL]` what the viewer sees: the object, diagram or action that explains the line, one focal point.
- `[BUILD]` stickman 2D, 3D, screen recording or photo (circle), so production can start straight from the script.
- `[ON SCREEN]` short text, labels and numbers (no em or en dashes).
- `[ENGAGE]` the question, guess, vote, takeaway or share prompt, spoken and shown on screen.
- `[CTA]` the action and the exact address, with when it appears on screen.
- `(pause)`, *emphasis* and tone notes where they help the voiceover.

**After the script, add**

- 3 alternative titles.
- Thumbnail text (2 to 4 words).
- 5 tags or keywords.
- Word count and estimated runtime.
- Voice plan: which lines can reuse MTG's recorded voice bank and which need new HeyGen lines (estimate the credits;
  ask MTG before spending any).
- Shorts plan: 2 or 3 vertical cut-downs of 20 to 60 seconds, each with its own hook, one prompt and one CTA.
- The job card: goal, stage, main address, comment prompt, share target.

Then ask: "Want edits, a different tone, a Shorts version, the storyboard, or a new topic?"

When the video is finished, deliver its posting pack (`references/posting-pack.md`).

## Rules

- **Accuracy.** Never invent statistics, quotes, studies, results or dates. Check facts against reliable sources. For
  MTG's own streams and sites, use his guide pages as the source and link the page. If something is debated, say so.
- **Money.** No income promises, no "get rich", no fake balances or testimonials. Show the risk next to every reward
  ("lower risk is not no risk"). Say "not financial advice". Where MTG earns from a referral link, say so plainly.
  This also keeps his YouTube and TikTok channels within platform rules.
- **Platform compliance.** Follow `references/compliance.md` on every platform: no guaranteed, quick or risk-free
  returns, no past returns of an opportunity, no recruitment pitches or compensation plans on social platforms, no
  paid draws or gambling, truthful titles and thumbnails, no rewards for engagement, original content every time, AI
  voice labelled, money interests disclosed, only owned or licensed media, privacy protected, adults only. Run
  `scripts/compliance_scan.py` on every script and caption. Never call a video "100% safe": say what was checked.
- **Learn from the winners, never copy them.** Copy the pattern (hook type, format, pacing, length, thumbnail
  pattern), never the property (script, wording, footage, music, thumbnail, characters, distinctive layout). Never
  re-upload anyone's video. Never claim a video is "top" or "viral" without numbers; when a platform hides its
  numbers, say so.
- **Psychology and manipulation topics** are for awareness and self protection only (spotting scams, FOMO, pressure
  tactics). Never use those tactics on the viewer.
- **Engagement is real.** Every prompt comes from the content and starts a real conversation; no empty "comment YES"
  bait (Facebook demotes it). Never ask for wallet addresses, recovery phrases, balances or account screenshots in
  comments. Only promise a reply, a part two or a link if MTG will really deliver it.
- **MTG's photo** is always a circle (see above).
- **Wording.** "Potential business partners" and "business partners", never "downline" or "recruits". "Training", not
  "demo". Say abbreviations in full the first time.
- **Write for the ear.** Simple words, varied sentence length, no walls of text, every line earns its place.
- **No em dashes or en dashes** anywhere: commas, colons, full stops or parentheses.
- **Analogies** say where they stop being accurate (a stokvel explains pooling, it does not prove a platform is safe).
- **Links.** Keep referral parameters (such as `?ref=mtg`) exactly as the live pages use them. Sites still being built
  get a tribe CTA until they are finished.

## From script to video

1. MTG approves the research brief and the script (and picks the build). Before he sees them, do the craft passes in
   `references/research.md` section 6 (5 hooks, 3 titles, 2 thumbnail concepts, read aloud, visual plan, fact check).
2. Storyboard the beats (mtg-creative-standard section 6).
3. Voice: reuse recorded lines first, new lines only with MTG's go-ahead (HeyGen "MTG Master Voice V2").
4. Build:
   - **3D:** the studio in `tools/video3d/` of the site's project. If it is not there yet, install it from this skill's
     `kit/video3d/` (see `references/3d-studio.md`). It makes word synced captions from the final audio, places MTG's
     circle photo, the brand bar and the closing track, and renders 16:9 and 9:16.
   - **The mascot:** Leo, MTG's mtgTribe lion (glasses, gold watch, MTG's evolution t-shirt) for hooks, reactions and
     endings, generated with HeyGen from his reference picture (`references/mascot.md`; about 15 credits per 8-second
     clip, so use him where he adds the most).
   - **Stick-figure stories:** the GrowKaz Stickman Studio plugin (`growkaz-stickman:make-2d-video`) when it is
     installed. MTG's standards win where its style rules differ (voice, circle photo, closing track).
   - **Tutorials:** real screen recordings with a visible cursor, arrows and highlights.
5. Preview to MTG, fix, render the Shorts cut-downs, and only publish after his approval.
6. Run the final compliance check (`references/compliance.md` section 5: video, thumbnail, title, description, links,
   platform labels) and hand over the posting pack (`references/posting-pack.md`) with the compliance record filled in.
7. Put the video where it helps: the matching website page (9:16 inside the slim gold phone frame, 16:9 inside the
   laptop frame, and the player closes itself when the video ends), YouTube, TikTok and WhatsApp status.
8. After posting: pin the comment, answer early comments, share it in the tribe, and turn the best questions into
   the next videos. Check the numbers weekly and make more of what works (playbook section 8).
