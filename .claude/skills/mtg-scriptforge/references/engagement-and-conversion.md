# Engagement and conversion playbook

MTG, 7 November 2026: "This is the business, the serious business to me." Every video must be fun, helpful,
educational and thought provoking, AND move the business: website traffic, sign-ups, sales, a growing team and a
growing MTG TRIBE. This playbook is how. It never overrides honesty: no income promises, no fake urgency, no
manipulation, risks shown plainly (mtg-creative-standard, mtg-hooks-standard).

## 1. Every video has a job

Choose before writing. One primary goal, at most one secondary.

| Goal | Success looks like | Typical video | Primary call to action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reach | new viewers, follows, shares | What If, Did You Know, myths, bridge topics | follow, share, join the tribe |
| Engagement | comments, saves, replies | questions, quizzes, "what would you do?" | a comment prompt |
| Traffic | visits to one page | explainers, calculator and tool trainings | that page's address |
| Leads | WhatsApp tribe joins, messages to MTG | honest checks, FAQs, "is it right for me?" | mtgtribe.com/group or a voice note to MTG |
| Sign-ups and sales | joins through MTG's links | sign-up training, first steps, fit checks | the stream's sign-up page |
| Team growth | business partners who duplicate | partner plans, lead with value, start your own tribe | partner plan page or the tribe |

## 2. The viewer journey

Every video moves the viewer one step forward, never from "just discovered you" straight to "sign up now".

| Stage | Videos | Next step offered |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | bridge topics (menu 1 to 5), Did You Know, myths | follow, comment, join the tribe |
| Learn | explainers, how it works, calculators | the guide page or tool |
| Trust | honest checks, risks, real tutorials, FAQ, answers to comments | the fit check page, or WhatsApp MTG a question |
| Decide and act | sign-up training, the first 72 hours, first steps | the sign-up page with MTG's link (disclosed) |
| Grow | partner plan, lead with value, start your own tribe, share kits | partner plan page, downloads and share kit |

**Default weekly mix** (adjust with the numbers in section 8): one long bridge video and its 2 or 3 Shorts; one long
direct video (explainer, honest check or tutorial) and its Shorts; a decision video every week or two (sign-up training,
fit check, or a Q&A built from comments); daily Shorts or WhatsApp status clips from the cut-downs. Most videos give
value; the direct asks ride on the trust that builds.

## 3. Length: as long as the job needs, not a second more

| Video | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Did You Know, myth, single fact (Shorts, Reels, TikTok) | 20 to 45 s | one fact, one prompt, an ending that loops into the start |
| Short explainer | 45 to 90 s | one idea, one call to action |
| WhatsApp status clip | under 60 s | cut from a longer video |
| What If and bridge videos (YouTube) | 3 to 5 min | ScriptForge structure |
| Explainers and honest checks | 4 to 8 min | chapters in the description |
| Step-by-step trainings | as long as the steps need | chapters, plus one clip per step |
| Deep dives, Q&A, replays (warm viewers) | 10 to 20 min | for people close to deciding |

The first 3 seconds decide a Short; the first 30 seconds decide a long video. Cut anything that does not teach, hold
attention or move the viewer forward.

## 4. The interaction layer

Write the interaction into the script with `[ENGAGE]` lines. Every prompt comes from the content and starts a real
conversation. MTG wants viewers to think, answer, share their thoughts and share the video.

**Long video (3 minutes or more): 3 or 4 moments**

1. **Prediction (first 45 seconds):** ask for a guess and pay it off later. "Before I tell you, how many zeros do you
   think were on that note? Comment your guess." It keeps people watching and fills the comments.
2. **Reflection (middle):** "Pause for a second. What would you have done?"
3. **Takeaway or vote (at the payoff):** "Comment one word: your biggest takeaway." or "A or B?"
4. **Share with someone specific (end):** "Send this to the friend who keeps all their savings in one place."

**Short: 1 prompt**, spoken and shown as on-screen text, plus an ending that loops back to the hook.

**Prompt library** (vary them; never the same prompt twice in a row in a series):

- Guess the number: "Comment your guess: 1, 10 or 100?"
- Rate yourself: "From 1 to 10, how safe is your wallet right now?"
- This or that: "Bank or self custody? Comment B or S."
- Finish the sentence: "Money is ___."
- Your experience: "Have you ever been scammed? Tell us what happened so others can learn." (No names, numbers or screenshots of accounts.)
- One-word takeaway.
- Pick the next video: "Comment 2 if you want part two." (Only if MTG will make it.)
- Ask for help: "Comment GUIDE and I'll reply with the link." (Only if someone will really reply.)
- Save it: "Save this for the day you need it."
- Share it: "Send this to someone who needs to hear it today."

**Platform note:** Facebook has said it shows fewer posts that bait comments ("comment YES if..."), so on Facebook ask
real questions tied to the content. Everywhere, a prompt must lead to a real conversation.

**Safety in comments:** never ask viewers to post wallet addresses, recovery phrases, balances, phone numbers or account
screenshots. Remind them often: "Never share your 12 words with anyone, not even me."

**After posting, keep the conversation going:**

- Pin a comment with the video's question and its main link.
- Reply to the early comments (first hour) and heart the good ones.
- Turn the best questions into the next video ("You asked, I answered"; on TikTok, reply with a video).
- Post the video in the WhatsApp tribe with a question to discuss, plus a ready-made status message members can share.

## 5. Website addresses: the right site at the right moment

- **Which site:** match the topic (`destinations.md`). One spoken main address plus the tribe. Every other link goes in
  the description, the pinned comment and the end card.
- **When:** the moment the viewer has just received value and has a natural next question, usually at the payoff or
  bridge (about 75 to 90% through). A long video may mention the page once earlier at a value moment ("every number is
  on mtgtribe.com/altruva").
- **How:** say it short and slowly ("mtgtribe dot com slash group"), show the exact address as a lower third for at
  least 3 seconds, and put the same address in the first two lines of the description, the pinned comment and the end
  card.
- **By platform:** TikTok and Instagram: show the short address and say "link in bio". YouTube: description, pinned
  comment, end screen, chapters. WhatsApp: the full link in the message.
- **Links:** exact live links with referral parameters unchanged (such as `?ref=mtg`). When MTG can earn from a link,
  say so: "If you join through my link, I may earn a reward." Sites still being built: send people to the tribe.
- **Short paths that exist:** mtgtribe.com, mtgtribe.com/group, mtgtribe.com/dashboard, mtgtribe.com/altruva,
  mtgtribe.com/jetup, mtgtribe.com/cntn, bellveraffiliates.com.

## 6. The call-to-action ladder

Per video: **one primary action** (from the goal) **plus the community action** (tribe or follow). Comment, save and
share prompts are part of the conversation, not extra sales asks.

End order: takeaway prompt, then the primary action with its address, then the tribe, then MTG's signature outro.
Never more than one "go to this address" in the last 20 seconds.

**Closing honestly:** people decide when it is clear. Say who it suits and who it does not, the risks, the fees, the
real steps, and offer a personal line: "Not sure if it fits you? Send me a WhatsApp voice note with your question."
No pressure, no fake deadlines, no income claims. That honesty is also what keeps MTG's accounts safe on every platform.

## 7. The posting pack

Every finished video ships with a posting pack (`posting-pack.md`): titles, description with the main link in the first
two lines, chapters, pinned comment, captions per platform, the WhatsApp tribe message and status text, thumbnail text,
end screen plan and the publish checklist.

## 8. Measure and improve

Track per video (YouTube Studio, TikTok analytics, or Metricool when it is connected): how many stay past 3 seconds
and 30 seconds, average view duration, comments, shares, saves, link clicks, new tribe members and sign-ups that week.
Keep a simple log. Make more of the topics and hooks that win; rewrite the hooks of the ones that do not and repost
the Shorts. Metricool can schedule posts, but only after MTG approves each one.
