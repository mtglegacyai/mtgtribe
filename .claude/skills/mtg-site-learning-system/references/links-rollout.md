# Rolling out MTG's links across a site

The moment MTG sends his referral or partner links, every call to action on that site carries them. A site with his links
is a converting machine; without them it only educates.

## Before you start

- Record each link in the site's PLAN.md: what it is for (for example the JetUP Partner link for joining, the BIX Card link
  for people who only want the card), the date, and which buttons use it.
- Open each link once to confirm it lands on the right sign-up page. Never invent, shorten or swap a link, and never use
  anyone else's.
- If a short, memorable address helps (for example mtgtribe.com/JetUpZoom), make a redirect page in the repository and test
  every spelling on the live site.

## Where the links go

1. The page where people start (the sign-up training): the first step's button is his link, with WhatsApp kept as the
   second option ("Stuck? Ask MTG").
2. One clear button at the end of every page that builds desire: home hero, checks, how it works, promo, partner plan,
   FAQ, product pages.
3. Every video end card and every PDF button for that step.
4. Product pages get their own product link (for example "Get your BIX Card").

## How each button should read

- Say what happens next and why it matters: "Start with MTG's Partner link" beats "Click here".
- One primary button per section; the tribe or WhatsApp button stays secondary.
- Keep the referral disclosure ("MTG's referral link") and the risk line close by. No income promises.
- Follow mtg-hooks-standard for the wording and mtg-creative-standard for the look and the tap targets.

## Search and AI search

Update titles, descriptions, HowTo and FAQ structured data where the new step adds value, and keep crawlable text for
every button's destination. Never stuff links or keywords.

## Check before showing MTG

Run the site's full test suite, the sideways-scroll check on phones, and a live link check that every button opens the
right destination. Preview to MTG, publish only with his approval, then test again on the live site.
