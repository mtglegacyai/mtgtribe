# Conversion copy: pages, sales letters, ads and traffic that turn visitors into action takers

Use for landing pages, sales pages and sales letters, order forms, offers, adverts and video ads, VSLs, social posts,
WhatsApp and email. Always with `voice-pass.md`, and inside MTG's rules: inform, never frighten (9 Nov 2026), a $ on
every amount (22 Nov 2026), the ClickBaitPays message (`tools/cbp/MESSAGE.md`), no income promises, no fake numbers.

Source: Marketing Skills by Corey Haines (MIT, `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` at 8fd2fcade2fd): the cro, copywriting,
offers, ad-creative, social and sms playbooks, adapted for MTG. Their honesty stance matches his: no fake countdown
timers, no invented statistics or testimonials.

## The page check (seven points, in order of impact)

1. **Five-second test.** Can a visitor say what this is and why it matters to them within five seconds? Benefit first,
   in the visitor's words, the single most important thing (not everything at once).
2. **Headline.** Outcome-focused and specific (a number, a time, a concrete detail), and it matches what sent the
   visitor here: the ad, the post or the video they just watched.
3. **One primary action.** Visible without scrolling on a phone, repeated at each decision point, and the button says
   the value: "Watch the 2-minute video", "Get my copy", "Join the WhatsApp tribe", never "Submit" or "Learn more".
   Secondary actions look secondary.
4. **Scannable.** Someone skimming gets the message from headings, buttons and pictures alone.
5. **Real proof, placed next to the action and after each claim.** MTG's own story (BellverCards), a check on chain,
   real redacted screens, real member counts if he gives them. Never invented.
6. **Objections answered on the page.** Is it worth the money? Will it work for someone like me? Is it hard? What if it
   doesn't work for me? Answer with FAQs, the steps shown openly, and one calm risk line.
7. **Friction removed.** Fewest form fields, a clear next step, fast on a phone, nothing that scrolls sideways.

Report a page review as: **Quick wins** (do now), **High-impact changes**, **Test ideas**, **Copy alternatives**.

## The claims rule

Write only claims the facts support. When MTG asks for a claim we cannot back up, don't write it and don't silently drop
it either: name the claim, say why it falls short, offer the closest honest version, and say what evidence would allow
the original (a screenshot of a payout, a written policy from the programme, an on-chain record).

## Honest offers and urgency

- Real urgency only: an event date, a real deadline, a real price change. Never a fake timer, never "only 3 spots left"
  when it isn't true. Fake urgency lifts once and costs trust for good.
- A strong offer has a clear core, real bonuses (MTG's PDF guide, his videos, the WhatsApp tribe), and honesty about what
  happens if it isn't for them.
- Expect realistic gains from a single change (10 to 40% better, multiplied, not added). Anyone promising 5 times more is
  selling something.
- Trust is the product in crypto. The upstream playbook warns that gold logos, "secret method" language and fake urgency
  look like a scam on a serious product. MTG's gold stays thin, restrained and premium, the copy stays calm and factual,
  and the proof stays real. That is how he stands apart from the hype in his niche.

## Ads and video ads

- **Ground every ad in something real.** Each concept traces to a source we can name: a question a prospect asked MTG on
  WhatsApp, a comment under his video, a review, a real result screen, an ad that already performed. No invented claims.
  MTG's own prospect questions ("is it Sonic AI copy trading?") are the best ad angles he owns.
- **Keep an inputs folder per campaign** when ads become regular: winning ads, real comments and questions, brand assets,
  dated output batches.
- Match the hook to how aware the viewer already is (never heard of it, knows the problem, comparing options, ready).
- Video ads: the first three seconds are their own edit (`video-story.md`). An ad may explain one concrete thing before
  inviting people to learn more; it must not promise a full tutorial and deliver only a sign-up button.
- Every ad passes mtg-scriptforge's compliance gate (YouTube, TikTok, Meta, X) before MTG sees it.

## Sales letters and VSLs

The floor for any sales video stays mtg-promo-video's sales edition (Explainer 6). On top of it: the seven-point page
check above applies to the letter or the VSL's landing page, every claim follows the claims rule, objections get their
own answers in order, and the action is one button repeated at each decision point.

## Traffic: posts, WhatsApp, email

- **The first line decides whether anyone reads the rest.** Hook shapes that work: curiosity ("[Outcome] usually comes down
  to [surprising cause]"), story ("Last week, [unexpected thing] happened"), value ("How to [outcome] without [pain]",
  "[Number] [things] that [outcome]"), contrarian ("I stopped [common practice] and [result]"). Fill each shape with a real
  fact, number or story, never a blank formula.
- **Post tells to avoid** (on top of `voice-pass.md`): "No X, no Y, no Z" lists, self-answered questions ("The result?
  3x faster."), "Say goodbye to", "Unlock the power of", "Take it to the next level", and one short sentence per line for the
  whole post. Break lines where a person would pause. MTG's description layout (label, blank line, 👉 link) stays.
- **Turn every long video into many posts.** From each tutorial, explainer, presentation or live Q&A, pick the 5 to 10
  moments that stand alone: a memorable line (15 to 60 s), a complete mini-story (60 to 90 s), one tactical tip (30 to 60 s),
  a surprising fact with its source. Each clip gets its own caption with context, subtitles (most people watch muted),
  and the right page link, spread over one to two weeks.
- **WhatsApp** (facts as of October 2026, check Meta's pricing page before quoting costs): in markets like South Africa
  WhatsApp beats SMS. The free Business app is fine for answering people by hand. Automation needs the Business Platform
  (through Meta or a provider): free-form messages only within 24 hours of the person's last message; outside that, only a
  Meta-approved template; anything promotional is a "marketing" template and is charged per message; blocks and reports
  lower the number's quality rating and can cut how many people it may message per day. So: invite people to message
  first (a click-to-WhatsApp button, a comment keyword), and never broadcast to people who did not ask.

## Not taken, and why

- "Remove exclamation marks": MTG's slogan and warm greetings keep theirs.
- "Show customer counts, testimonials and logos": only real ones MTG supplies; never placeholders.
- The SaaS parts (trials, paywalls, churn, pricing tiers): not MTG's business model. Read them upstream only if one fits.
