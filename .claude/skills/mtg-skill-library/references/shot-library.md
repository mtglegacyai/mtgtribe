# Shot library: MTG's named cinematic looks

Use for video covers and thumbnails, social previews, ad and reel inserts, promo b-roll and website hero pictures: any
visual built around one hero object. Always with `design-review.md` and MTG's covers and thumbnails rules
(`tools/youtube-thumbnails/`, the play-button band, the 1200 x 630 preview formula).

Source: video #002 ("Type this, get this" reel, sent 27 Nov 2026) and the public lists behind it, read on 27 Nov 2026:
`bhaiyasingh45/chatgpt-slash-image-commands` (at bd2c8e6; no licence file, so ideas only, nothing copied), the Slash
Image Commander sales page and a Google Flow prompt guide. Every one of them says the same: **these "slash commands" are
not features of ChatGPT, Gemini or Flow.** They are shorthand words, and a one-word command gives unreliable results; a
full description of subject, action, setting, camera, light and style works.

## How MTG's commands work

Saved here, the looks below become real commands for MTG. When he types `/<look> <subject>` in any chat or session that
has this library (for example `/blueprint Bellver Card` or `/explodedview ClickBaitPays`), expand it into the full
recipe: the look's recipe below, plus

- **subject:** exactly what he named, with real logos only (the programme's official logo as the sites already use it);
- **brand:** dark ground, thin gold-titanium accents, crisp premium light, nothing cheap or cluttered;
- **format:** 16:9 cover (play button in the middle), 9:16 reel or cover (clear band y 780 to 1140 on 1080 x 1920),
  1200 x 630 social preview (headline, the visual, the address pill);
- **words:** one short headline at most, set as real text on top in our own fonts, never letters drawn by an AI model;
- **must not show:** anything in "Honest pictures" below.

Then show MTG the expanded recipe and the way to make it (free or credits) before producing anything.

The reel's own spellings work too: `/3dblueprint` is `/blueprint`, `/orbit_sweep` is `/orbit`.

## Making it: free first, credits only with MTG's OK

- **Free, exact brand:** our HTML engine with three.js renders `/orbit`, `/explodedview`, `/blueprint` and `/bullettime`
  frame by frame with real logos and sharp text on top (the 3D studio in mtg-promo-video and mtg-scriptforge;
  HyperFrames blocks in `video-story.md`). No credits, exact colours, nothing invented.
- **AI generators, credits:** HeyGen `create_image` for a still, `image_to_video` or `text_to_video` (5 to 30 s) for
  motion; Google Gemini, Veo and Flow; others. Ask MTG before spending credits. Send the full recipe, never the bare
  command. Google's tools add a visible sparkle in the corner (it can be switched off in Gemini and Flow settings) and an
  invisible SynthID mark that stays: never use a watermark remover.

## Honest pictures (MTG's rules, always)

- No fake balances, payouts, earnings screens, app features or member counts. A product screen in a shot is a real
  screen, redacted.
- No money raining, no arrows shooting up, no luxury-car promises: that is the "course-bro" look that reads as a scam
  in his niche (`conversion-copy.md`).
- No other companies' products or logos (the reel used AirPods, a Rolex and Puma), except the programmes' own official
  logos as his sites already use them.
- No AI-made "MTG": his approved avatar only, and no photo of him in tutorials or on their thumbnails.
- Realistic AI people or scenes get the platform's AI label (YouTube, TikTok, Meta): mtg-scriptforge's compliance gate.

## Not taken, and why

- "100+ commands": a long list of single words is the lead magnet, not the value. We keep seven looks with full
  recipes, chosen for MTG's subjects.
- The reel's subjects (earbuds, a luxury watch, a phone, sneakers, perfume): other companies' products.
- Presenting the commands as built-in features: untrue, and his honesty rules forbid it.
