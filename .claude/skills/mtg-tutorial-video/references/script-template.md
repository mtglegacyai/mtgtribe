# Script template (copy, then fill in the part)

Banked lines are marked (bank: file, key). Everything else is one new HeyGen request per part. Write names the way they
are said: "Jet Up", "MTG tribe dot com slash jet up", "I B Portal", "Copy X", "twelve".

| # | Beat | Line | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Greeting | "Hi, my name is MTG, Your Digital Architect." (bank: t1 `hi`) | Circle photo large, part chip, title |
| 2 | Greeting | "Thank you for taking the time to watch this video." (bank: tutorial/p1v2 `n_thanks`) | Name under the photo |
| 3 | In this video | "In this video, I'll show you how to [goal], step by step, on your phone." | Photo shrinks top left, "IN THIS VIDEO" panel |
| 4 | Overview | 2 or 3 sentences naming the 6 to 9 things they will do, in order | Each item pops in on its word |
| 5 | Pace | "Take your time." "You can pause any step and follow along with me." (bank: t1) | Pause chip |
| 6 | Steps | One short line per step, exact on-screen labels; tips for errors he hit | Step card, window, ring, tap, chips, tip cards |
| 7 | Recap | "Let's recap what we covered in this video." then 3 or 4 sentences in the past tense | Same list, each item ticks on its word |
| 8 | Next | "In the next video, part N, I'll show you how to [next goal]." + one line with its 2 or 3 actions | NEXT VIDEO card, 3 numbered actions |
| 9 | Links | "Everything I showed you, plus my links, is at MTG tribe dot com slash jet up." (bank: t3 `links`) | URL pill |
| 10 | Fair play | "Invited by someone else? Use their link. This video is for every team, so feel free to share it." (bank: p1v3 `l_every`) | Gold-edged note |
| 11 | Save, share | "Save this video, so it's here when you need it." "Share it with someone who's ready to start." (bank: t3) | Line under the URL |
| 12 | Outro | Signature outro, all banked (t3 `o_fin` to `o_lgtc`) | Circle photo, message line by line, like/share/comment icons on the words, YOU ARE THE BEST, brand line, slogan, risk line |

Where the part sends people to sign up (usually step 1 of part 1), use the fair-play pair (bank: p1v3 `l_ifme`, `l_other`,
adjust the URL and button label for other sites):
"If I invited you, go to MTG tribe dot com slash jet up, and tap any Join Jet Up button."
"If someone else invited you, use their Partner link instead, so you join their team."

Never: "training" in the greeting, "downline" or "recruits", income promises, em or en dashes, a one-time code, a balance.
