Ranks and Lucky Tickets

The last two shares, 5% each: rank pools for qualified members, and a prize draw every time the pool reaches 5,000 USDT.

Nexus and Legacy Crown250 winning tickets per drawExplainer 5, 2:41

Two ranks

Ranks
RankPackageTo qualify (from the ALTRUVA presentation)
NexusBuilder, $50Hold at least $50, plus 2 directs of $50 or more within 3 days
Legacy CrownElite, $100Hold $100, plus 2 directs of $100 or more within 3 days

The 3 days start when you join: see Your first 72 hours. Rank pools are shared among everyone who holds that rank. You claim your share from Overview, like the global leg and matching. The split between the two pools isn't shown in the app, so we don't state it.

Lucky Tickets

Each ticket enters one draw, then it expires. A prize goes to your wallet in full: no split, no reinvest.

Is this a jackpot?

No. Think of a raffle at a school fun day: everyone gets tickets, a few win small prizes. Most tickets don't win, so never count on a prize.
