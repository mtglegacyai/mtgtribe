What is Canton Coin?

Canton Coin (CC) is the coin of the Canton Network, a blockchain built for banks and big financial firms. Every transaction on the network is paid for in CC, a bit like a toll token on a private road for banks.

1

Set up your Canton wallet

Step 1 of 6

Goal: have your own Canton address ready before you buy

Canton Coin lives on its own blockchain. MetaMask, Trust Wallet and Phantom cannot hold real CC. Use a Canton wallet from the table above, downloaded from its official site only.
  1. Open 5N Loop (easiest, works in your browser) or another Canton wallet: Console, Nightly or Bron.
  2. Sign up with your email or Google and create your passkey.
  3. Copy your Canton address (also called your party ID) and save it in your notes.
  4. Back up your passkey, or write your seed phrase on paper. Never keep it as a screenshot.

Success check: your wallet shows a CC balance of 0 and your party ID.

2

Buy USDT with rand on VALR

Step 2 of 6

Goal: turn rand into USDT, a US dollar stablecoin

  1. Open the VALR app (or Luno) and deposit rand from your bank by EFT.
  2. Tap Buy, choose USDT, enter your rand amount and confirm.
Start small: send a small test amount through every step first. You pay a trading fee here and a network fee in the next step.

OPEN YOUR VALR ACCOUNT

Success check: USDT shows in your VALR balance.

3

Send USDT to an exchange that lists CC

Step 3 of 6

Goal: get your USDT onto an exchange where Canton Coin trades

  1. Open an account at Kraken, Bybit, KuCoin, Gate or MEXC and finish the ID check. VALR and Luno do not list CC yet.
  2. On that exchange, tap Deposit, choose USDT and pick a network, for example TRON (TRC20) or BNB Smart Chain.
  3. Copy the deposit address.
  4. In VALR, tap Withdraw, choose USDT, paste the address and choose the same network.
  5. Confirm and wait a few minutes.
The network must match on both sides. Sending on the wrong network can lose your funds.

Success check: USDT arrives on the exchange.

4

Buy CC with a limit order

Step 4 of 6

Goal: swap your USDT for Canton Coin at a price you choose

  1. Search for CC and open the CC/USDT market (Kraken also has CC/USD).
  2. Choose Limit. A limit order means: buy, but never above this price.
  3. Enter your price (about the current price) and the amount.
  4. Check the total, then tap Buy CC.
Think of a limit order as telling a shop assistant "I'll take it, but only up to R50" instead of "ring it up, whatever it costs".

Success check: CC shows in your exchange balance.

5

Withdraw CC to your own wallet

Step 5 of 6

Goal: move your Canton Coin into the wallet only you control

  1. On the exchange, tap Withdraw and choose CC.
  2. Choose the Canton network.
  3. Paste your party ID from step 1 and check the first and last characters.
  4. Send a small test amount first. When it arrives, send the rest.
  5. Leave a little CC in your wallet. It pays the network fees when you swap.

Success check: your wallet shows your CC. You now hold Canton Coin yourself. 🎉

6

Optional: swap on a Canton DEX

Step 6 of 6

Goal: trade straight from your wallet, with no exchange in between

  1. Open OneSwap, Canton's permissionless DEX (decentralised exchange).
  2. Tap Connect Wallet, choose your wallet and approve.
  3. Pick a pair (CC/USDCx or CC/CBTC), enter the amount, check the price impact and the 0.3% fee, then confirm.

Stay safe

  • Only download wallets from their official sites. Ignore any "Canton Coin on Ethereum" or "CC on BNB" token: real CC lives on Canton.
  • Nobody genuine will ask for your seed phrase or passkey. If you lose them, nobody can recover your coins.
  • Always match the network when you send, and send a small test amount first.
  • CC is volatile. It is about 50% below its February 2026 high, and new CC is created every day.
  • Only use money you can afford to lose, and keep records for SARS.

Questions people ask

Can I buy Canton Coin from South Africa?

Yes. Buy USDT with rand on VALR or Luno, send it to an exchange that lists CC (Kraken, Bybit, KuCoin, Gate or MEXC), buy CC there, then withdraw it on the Canton network to your own Canton wallet.

Can I buy Canton Coin on VALR or Luno?

Not directly, as of 1 November 2026. Neither lists CC, so you use USDT as the bridge to an exchange that does.

Can MetaMask or Trust Wallet hold Canton Coin?

No. Canton is not an Ethereum style (EVM) blockchain, so you need a Canton wallet such as 5N Loop, Console, Nightly, Bron or Zoro, or a Ledger or Cypherock hardware wallet.

What is the best Canton wallet for beginners?

5N Loop is the simplest. It runs in your browser, uses a passkey instead of a seed phrase and takes about 2 minutes to set up. For bigger amounts, consider a hardware wallet.

Which decentralised exchanges trade Canton Coin?

OneSwap (CC/USDCx and CC/CBTC) and Cantex (CC/USDC). Both are new, so start with small swaps.

Is Canton Coin the same as CNTN?

No. Canton Coin (CC) is the coin of Canton Network. CNTN is Canton Strategic Holdings, a Nasdaq company that holds CC. You buy CC on crypto exchanges and CNTN through a stockbroker.

Is Canton Coin a good investment?

Nobody can promise that. Banks and big financial firms are testing the network, but CC is about 50% below its February 2026 high and more CC is still created than burned. Only use money you can afford to lose.

Do I pay tax on Canton Coin?

SARS treats crypto gains as taxable, including crypto to crypto swaps. Keep a record of every buy, swap and withdrawal, and speak to a registered tax practitioner if you are unsure.
