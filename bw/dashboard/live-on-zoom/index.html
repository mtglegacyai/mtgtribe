How do I get the Zoom link?

The Zoom links are only shared inside the MTG TRIBE WhatsApp group, never in public. Ask the person who invited you to add you to the group, join the group, or message MTG on WhatsApp and ask for the link.

What time is it where I live?

Every time on this page is South African time (SAST, UTC+2, no daylight saving). The time zone table shows Nigeria, the United Kingdom, Canada and US East and Australia, plus a Your time column worked out from your own device.

Which language is each session in?

Monday to Friday at 14:00 the sessions are in English, and questions are welcome in any language, like isiZulu. In the evenings: Monday 20:00 isiXhosa; Tuesday and Wednesday 20:00 English; Thursday 20:00 isiZulu; Friday 19:00 Sesotho.

Can I join from my phone?

Yes. Zoom works on phones, tablets and computers. Install the Zoom app on your phone, then tap the link when it is shared in the group.
