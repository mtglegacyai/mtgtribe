Welcome

Hi, my name is MTG, Your Digital Architect. Thank you for taking the time to watch this video.

In this video, I'll show you how to set up your SafePal wallet for Bitcoin Wealth, step by step, on your phone. We'll download the official app, create a brand new wallet, and back it up two ways. Then we'll clean it up, so it shows only the three coins you need. And I'll show you what it costs to get started. Take your time. You can pause any step and follow along with me.

  1. Why a Web3 wallet?

    Why a Web3 wallet? A Web3 wallet is an app where you hold your own crypto, with your own keys. No bank, no company and no admin holds it for you. Bitcoin Wealth pays wallet to wallet, straight into yours. In this video, we use SafePal. Other Web3 wallets, like MetaMask, work too.

Part 1Download the official SafePal app

  1. Leo, the mascot: First, download the official app. Only from safepal.com.

  2. 1

    Go to safepal.com

    Open your browser and go to safepal.com, the official website. Always download apps from the official website, never from a link someone sends you. Scroll down, and tap Download App.

  3. 2

    Get it on Google Play

    On Android, tap Get it on Google Play. On iPhone, tap App Store.

  4. 3

    Check it, then Install

    Before you install, check two things: the developer, SafePal Wallet, and more than 5 million downloads. That's how you know it's the real app. Then tap Install.

  5. 4

    Decline offer, then Open

    If Google Play shows you an offer, tap Decline offer. Stay focused. When it's done, tap Open.

  6. 5

    Create New Wallet

    Tap Create New Wallet. We're making a brand new wallet, just for Bitcoin Wealth.

  7. 6

    Set your 6-digit password

    Create a 6-digit password, then type it again to confirm. It unlocks SafePal on this phone.

  8. 7

    Your Wallet is Ready!

    Your wallet is ready. Read this with me: Secure your wallet by saving your mnemonic phrase. This unique set of words is your only key to access your funds. SafePal cannot store or recover it for you. Your mnemonic phrase is your 12 secret recovery words. Whoever has them, has your wallet. This is the most important part of this video, so save it now. It'll be here when you do it yourself.

Part 2Back up your wallet, two ways

  1. Leo, the mascot: Now back it up, two ways. Never skip this.

  2. 8

    Back Up Now

    Tap Back Up Now. Never skip this. There are two ways to back up, and we'll do both.

  3. 9

    Back up to Google Drive

    First, Back up to Google Drive. Choose your Google account, then enter your 6-digit password.

  4. 10

    Create a backup password

    Now create a backup password. Easy for you to remember, hard for anyone to guess: at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers. Only tap the eye if nobody is around you. SafePal can't reset this password, so write it down. Your backup file is encrypted on your phone. Encrypted means locked with your password, so nobody can read it without it. Then it goes to your Google Drive. SafePal doesn't keep a copy. Tap Next.

  5. 11

    Read, tick, Start Backup

    Read these before you tick them. The backup password is not your wallet password. It only restores your wallet from cloud storage, which means your Google Drive, online. It can't be changed or recovered. If you lose it, you can't restore from Google Drive. Tick all three, and tap Start Backup.

  6. 12

    First backup: done

    Done. Your first backup is safe.

  7. 13

    Name it: Bitcoin Wealth

    Now give your wallet a clear name. Tap the wallet name at the top, then the three dots next to your wallet, and the pencil. Type Bitcoin Wealth, and tap Confirm.

  8. 14

    Never share your private key

    On this page you'll also see Export Private Key. Never share your private key with anyone. Like your 12 words, it opens your wallet.

  9. 15

    Back up manually

    Now the second backup, on paper. Tap Backup, then Back up manually. Read these three: losing your mnemonic phrase means losing your assets for good. Deleting the app deletes the wallet data. And back up somewhere private, with no person and no camera around you. Tick them, tap Understood, and enter your 6-digit password.

  10. Write your 12 words on paper

    SafePal now shows your 12 words, and it hides this screen from recordings, to protect you. Write the 12 words on paper, in order, and keep them offline. Never take a screenshot, never copy them, never store them online, and never send them to anyone. Then tap Next, and tap the words in the same order, to prove you wrote them down correctly. Made a mistake? Clear them and try again. When they're right, tap Verify.

  11. 16

    Backup completed

    Backup completed. Two green ticks: Google Drive, and manual. Well done. Most people skip this part, and you didn't.

Part 3Clean up your wallet

  1. Leo, the mascot: Now, clean up your wallet. You only need three coins.

  2. 17

    Manage Coins: keep BNB

    Now let's clean up your wallet, so it shows only what Bitcoin Wealth needs. On the wallet home, tap the three dots next to Search, then Manage Coins. Turn off every coin except BNB, BEP20. BNB pays the small network fees, called gas.

  3. 18

    Add USDT and BTCB (BEP20)

    Tap the plus, search USDT, and turn on USDT, BEP20. BEP20 means it runs on BNB Smart Chain, Binance's blockchain. Then search BTC, and turn on BTCB, BEP20.

  4. What is BTCB?

    BTCB is Bitcoin's value on BNB Smart Chain. It follows the price of Bitcoin one to one, and it's what Bitcoin Wealth runs on.

  5. 19

    3 coins: BNB, BTCB, USDT

    Back on the wallet home, you should see just three coins: BNB, BTCB and USDT. If yours looks like mine, comment DONE.

  6. For builders: a website like this one

    Builders, a website like this one, with your own name and your links, does the explaining for you. BW stands for Bitcoin Wealth, and you can order yours at mtgtribe.com/bwcopy.

Part 4What it costs to start

  1. Leo, the mascot: So, what does it cost to start?

  2. 20

    Bitcoin on 14 Nov 2026

    So what does it cost to get started? I checked on CoinGecko on 14 November 2026. Bitcoin was about $67,200. The minimum to start Bitcoin Wealth is 0.001 Bitcoin, 1 thousandth of a Bitcoin. That was about $67. And the 0.001 is a once-off. You never pay again to move up to the next slots.

  3. 21

    About R1,300 to start

    That day, $1 was about R16.98. I'd prepare about $76, around R1,300. That covers the 0.001, the fees from your bank to VALR, and from VALR to SafePal, and a little BNB for gas. No VALR or Binance account yet? Tap the buttons below this video to open one. I use both. It's smart to have a backup, just like having more than one income stream.

  4. Prices change every day

    Prices change every day. The entry is fixed in Bitcoin, so when Bitcoin is lower, you need fewer rands to start, and when it's higher, you need more.
