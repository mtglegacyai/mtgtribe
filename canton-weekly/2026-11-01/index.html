This week in one paragraph

Canton Coin is at $0.0962 (about R1.64), down about 5% this week and 21% this month. The trend is still making lower highs and price sits below its key moving averages, with support at $0.091 to $0.094 being tested. The new burn rules only start around Q1 2027, so supply still grows. CNTN stock rose about 6% to $1.98 on news of tokenized shares.

Fundamentals

Score: Mixed

  • 🔥 Burn vs mint: universal burn (CIP-0129) was approved on 21 October but only starts around Q1 2027. Until then the network creates about twice as much CC as it burns.
  • 🏦 Banks: institutions use the network (DTCC tokenization, Treasury repo trials), but those deals settle in USDC or tokenized assets, not CC.
  • 🧩 Ecosystem: new grant for private supply proofs (Ligero), CBTC trading on Rocky, 13 Super Validators. No new unlocks or major listings this week.
