ClickBaitPays dashboard: every tutorial, in order

Watch the site tour first, then follow the tutorials from 1 to 7. Each one shows one job in the ClickBaitPays back office, click by click, recorded by MTG on his own account.

Your daily routine

  1. Log in at clickbaitpays.me/login.php and view the login ad (30 seconds)
  2. Open Click Ads (or tap Start Clicking)
  3. For each ad: View & Earn, Open Ad, wait 15 seconds, Confirm & Earn
  4. Done for today when it says Click day complete. New clicks from 7:00 AM (South Africa)
