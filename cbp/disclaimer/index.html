Disclaimer and privacy

Plain words about this guide, the links on it and your privacy. Last updated 22 November 2026.

An independent guide

This is MTG's own guide to ClickBaitPays, made to educate. It is not the official ClickBaitPays website (that is clickbaitpays.me) and it is not run by ClickBaitPays. Rules, prices and features can change: the official site always has the final word.

The Join buttons carry MTG's referral link

Every Join button opens the official sign-up page with MTG as your sponsor (code mtgtraffic). If you join through it, MTG receives 10% of the ad campaigns you buy, as the platform's referral rules describe. Your prices do not change. Invited by someone else? Please use their link instead.

About the figures

Prices, activation fees, pay per click, clicks a day and campaign totals are the platform's own figures, read in the ClickBaitPays back office on 21 November 2026. Campaign totals assume all 12 click days. They describe how the platform pays; they are not a promise of what you will earn.

Advertising, not an investment

ClickBaitPays is an advertising platform: you buy advertising for your link and are paid for viewing other members' ads. Nothing on this site is financial advice. Results depend on the ads you view and on the platform, and nothing is guaranteed. Start with an amount you are comfortable with and only use money you can afford to lose.

Privacy: what this site stores

Nothing personal. There are no accounts, forms that store data, or tracking cookies. The calculator runs in your own browser. Your browser remembers only whether you collapsed the dashboard menu. YouTube videos load from youtube-nocookie.com only when you press play. The copy order form opens WhatsApp with your message; nothing is saved on this site.
