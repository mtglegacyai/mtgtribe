Quick answer

To buy CNTN: open a Cash account with a broker that offers US stocks, such as Interactive Brokers (most countries) or, in the US, apps like Robinhood. Verify your ID and address, fund it in US dollars, search CNTN on NASDAQ, place a limit order, then check that CNTN appears in your Portfolio.

What is CNTN?

CNTN is the Nasdaq ticker of Canton Strategic Holdings, a US-listed company that holds Canton Coin (CC) and runs a Super Validator on the Canton Network. Because it holds so much CC, its share price tends to follow the Canton Coin price.

1

Open your Interactive Brokers account

Step 1 of 6

Goal: submit your application

Type interactivebrokers.com into your browser yourself. Do not click ads or search results: lookalike sites exist.
  1. Go to interactivebrokers.com and tap Open Account.
  2. Enter your email, create a username and password, then type in the code Interactive Brokers emails you.
  3. Choose an Individual account.
  4. Enter your details: your country of residence, your address, and your tax residency with your tax number.
  5. Answer the experience and finance questions honestly. Being new to stocks is fine.
  6. Answer each settings screen using the choices below.
  7. Read the agreements, sign with your name and tap Submit.

Success check: you get an email that your application was received, and logging in shows it as pending or asks for documents.

2

Verify your identity

Step 2 of 6

Goal: get your account approved

  1. Log in at interactivebrokers.com and open the task list it shows you.
  2. Upload proof of identity: a clear photo of your ID card or passport, all four corners visible, no glare.
  3. Upload proof of address: a utility bill or bank statement less than 12 months old, with the same name and address as your application.
  4. If asked, complete the selfie or video check in the app.
  5. Wait for the approval email. This usually takes a few business days.
Tip: your name and ID number must match your application exactly. A small difference is the most common reason for delays.
Get the app now: download IBKR GlobalTrader (simplest for buying shares) or IBKR Mobile, only from the official Google Play or Apple App Store.

Success check: you get an email saying your account is approved and open.

3

Send money from your bank

Step 3 of 6

Goal: get US dollars into your account

Always start in Interactive Brokers, never at your bank. Interactive Brokers only shows its bank details after you tell it a deposit is coming.

Part A: tell Interactive Brokers a deposit is coming

  1. Log in on the website and tap Transfer & Pay, then Transfer Funds, then Make a Deposit.
  2. Choose the currency (USD is simplest) and the method, usually Wire.
  3. Enter the amount and continue to get the transfer instructions.
  4. Save the instructions: bank name, SWIFT code, account number and reference. Copy them exactly.

Part B: send it from your bank

  1. In your banking app, choose international payment (also called a SWIFT or wire payment).
  2. Paste the details from Part A. The reference is what links the money to you, so do not skip it.
  3. Send US dollars. Your bank converts your local money.
  4. Confirm and keep your proof of payment.
Money usually arrives in 1 to 4 business days. Banks charge a transfer fee and a margin on the exchange rate, so send a small test amount first.
Sent another currency? A Cash account must hold dollars to buy a US share, so convert first. In GlobalTrader: Home, then Convert, choose USD, enter the amount, tap Preview, then Convert.

Success check: your cash balance shows US dollars.

4

Find CNTN

Step 4 of 6

Goal: open the right stock, not a lookalike

  1. Open the app and tap the search bar.
  2. Type CNTN.
  3. Tap the result that shows Canton Strategic Holdings on NASDAQ.
  4. Check the price looks sensible: it should be close to the CNTN price on Google or Nasdaq.com.

Success check: the stock page shows CNTN, Canton Strategic Holdings, NASDAQ, with a recent price.

5

Place a limit order

Step 5 of 6

Goal: buy CNTN at a price you choose

A Limit order says "buy, but never above this price". A Market order buys at whatever price is offered right now, which can be a bad surprise on a small stock.

  1. On the CNTN page, tap Buy.
  2. Order type: Limit.
  3. Limit price: about the current price.
  4. Quantity: the number of shares. Check the estimated total fits your cash.
  5. Time in force: Day (cancels at the end of the session if not filled) or GTC, Good till Cancel.
  6. Tap Preview, check the commission and total, then Submit.

6

Check your portfolio

Step 6 of 6

Goal: see your shares in your account

  1. Tap Portfolio.
  2. Find CNTN in your positions.
  3. Check that the number of shares and your average price match your order.
Keep your trade confirmations. Most tax offices tax gains when you sell, so you will need your buy price and date.

Success check: CNTN appears in your Portfolio with your share count. You now own shares in Canton Strategic Holdings. 🎉

Stay safe

  • Your broker will never ask for your password or codes on WhatsApp, Telegram or by phone. Turn on two-factor login.
  • Only use the broker's official website and app stores. Ignore "agents" who offer to open or fund an account for you.
  • CNTN is a small, volatile stock. Its 52-week range runs from $1.66 to $6.89, and its value tends to follow the Canton Coin price.
  • The CNTN prices on this page are from 30 October 2026 and may be out of date. Check today's price on Nasdaq.com before you buy.
  • If you invest from outside the US, your result also moves with your currency against the US dollar.
  • Only use money you can afford to lose, and start with a small test amount.

Questions people ask

Can I buy CNTN stock outside the US?

Yes. CNTN is listed on Nasdaq, and most international brokers that offer US stocks, such as Interactive Brokers, let residents of most countries buy it. You open an account, fund it in US dollars and place an order for CNTN.

Where can I buy CNTN in the US?

Most US brokers offer Nasdaq stocks. CNTN has a stock page on Robinhood and SoFi, for example. Search CNTN in your app and place a limit order.

Can I buy CNTN in South Africa?

Yes, through Interactive Brokers. Send US dollars from your SA bank under your R2 million single discretionary allowance, then buy CNTN on Nasdaq. We could not confirm that EasyEquities offers CNTN.

Is CNTN the company that built Canton Network?

No. CNTN is Canton Strategic Holdings, a Nasdaq company that holds Canton Coin (CC) and runs a Super Validator on the network. Canton Network was built by Digital Asset, a private company.

How much money do I need to start?

A Cash account at Interactive Brokers has no minimum deposit. You need enough for at least one share (about $1.98 at the 30 October 2026 close) plus commission. Banks charge a fee on every international transfer, so very small transfers are expensive.

Can I pay in my own currency?

Your bank converts your money to US dollars when you send an international payment. If you send another currency, convert it to US dollars inside the app before buying, because a Cash account must hold dollars to buy a US share.

Do I pay tax on CNTN shares?

In most countries, gains on shares are taxed when you sell. Keep your trade confirmations with your buy price and date, and speak to a registered tax practitioner if you are unsure.

What are tokenized CNTN shares?

Canton Strategic Holdings announced plans for its shares to also trade in tokenized form on the Canton Network through TSV LLC, from as early as 2 November 2026, under the SEC's innovation exemption. Only cleared participants can trade, and access for non-US investors is not yet clear. This guide covers the regular Nasdaq shares.
