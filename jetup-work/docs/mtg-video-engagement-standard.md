# MTG video engagement standard (all projects, from 26 Oct 2026)

MTG's goal: videos watched from start to end, rewatched, saved, shared, commented on and liked; they speak life into people, revive hope and dreams, make the complicated simple and visual, let viewers see themselves winning, and grow a culture of each one teach one. Applies to every video script in every project.

## 1. Identity call to action ("If you... then...")
Pattern: "If you [are / like / believe in X], [action] this video, because [reason that helps them]."
Identities: Bitcoin lovers, crypto lovers, Crypto-Preneurs, multiple streams of income, diversified portfolios, smart contract platforms, action takers, leaders, team builders, TikTok live streamers, dreamers.
2 or 3 identity lines per video, matched to the topic.

## 2. Save, more often than any other call to action
At least once in the middle (just before the most important step: "Save this now so it's here when you do it yourself") and once at the end. Like, share, comment and follow come too, each with its on-screen icon as it is said.

## 3. Speak to how people feel
| Viewer | Line that meets them |
|---|---|
| Burned by past losses | "Your caution is wisdom, not weakness. This time, check every step yourself." |
| Sceptic or doubter | "Don't believe me. Watch the numbers, then check them yourself." |
| Hopeless | "Maybe it didn't work because nobody showed you how. Let's do it together." |
| Dreamer | "That dream needs a first step, not a perfect plan. This is step one." |
| Go-getter | "You're ahead because you act. Save this so your next move is faster." |
| TikTok live streamer | "Next time someone asks this on your live, send them this video." |
| Leader or team builder | "Don't explain this ten times. Share it once and let your team learn." |
| Positive people | "Pass this to one person who needs it today." |
Uplift lines: each one teach one; write your own testimonial; be the proof; change the narrative in your community; picture yourself teaching this next month.

## 4. Genuine compliments (one or two per video, only where earned)
Still watching halfway; did a hard step; saving the video; shared by someone who believes in them; finished the video.

## 5. Questions that invite comments (one or two per video)
- Easy one-word answers: "Comment YES if this helped." "Type DONE when your wallet is set up."
- Choices: "Tutorial or explainer next? Comment T or E." "Which got you: the global leg or the matching? Comment 1 or 2."
- Next video requests: "What should I make for you next? Tell me below." "Which tutorial, explainer or topic do you want next?"
- Their story: "What stopped you from starting before? I read every comment." "Where are you watching from?"
- Teaching: "Who will you teach this to? Tag them."
Placement: one light question early or mid video, one request question at the end before the signature outro. Pin a matching comment; reply to comments; turn the most requested topics into the next videos and credit the request on screen ("You asked, here it is").

### 5a. Every comment prompt must pass this test (MTG, 4 Nov 2026)
MTG rejected "Tell me in the comments: which step took you the longest?" as weak: it asks for effort, gives nothing back and has no reason to answer now. Never use low-stakes "tell me which step..." or "let me know what you think" lines again.
A strong comment prompt has all three:
1. One easy word or number to type (DONE, READY, a Part number, a yes or no).
2. A reason that helps the viewer (help when stuck, the next video built on their answer, being counted among the action takers).
3. A moment to do it now (as soon as they finish, while it is fresh, today).
Strong patterns:
- Progress plus help: "Comment DONE as soon as your account is set up. Stuck? Comment the Part number instead, and the step most of you ask about becomes my next video."
- Identity: "If you're an action taker, comment READY, and come back to type DONE when it's finished."
- Choice that shapes the next video: "Want the withdrawal training next, or how to read your dashboard? Comment W or D."
Show the word they should type on screen, big, as it is said.

## 5b. Real urgency, never fake (MTG, 4 Nov 2026)
When something genuinely is time-limited (a promo, a launch window), say so plainly and use it as a reason to act and to share: "A promo is a promo: it won't be here forever." Always honest: say it was running when the video was made, never invent a deadline or a countdown, tell viewers where to confirm it is still on, and keep the rules and risks next to it.

## 6. Flow of every video
1 hook a feeling (first 5 seconds) > 2 simple, visual, one idea at a time > 3 save prompt before the key step > 4 one compliment after effort > 5 lift them up > 6 question for the comments > 7 identity call to action (like, save, share, comment, follow) > 8 signature outro word for word.

## 7. Inside the video (added 26 Oct 2026)
- Open loop early: give a reason to stay to the end ("Stay to the end, the last step saves you fees").
- Text hook on screen in the first second (most people scroll with sound off).
- Loop ending where it fits: the last line leads back into the opening, so the video replays.
- Checklist frame worth pausing, screenshotting and saving ("Before you join: wallet, USDT, BNB for fees").
- Scam and safety warnings ("Nobody from support will ever ask for your recovery phrase").
- Local touch now and then: a short isiZulu phrase, rands next to dollars, everyday South African examples (taxis, stokvels).

## 8. After posting (added 26 Oct 2026)
- Reply to comments with a video (TikTok "reply with video") and turn common questions into content.
- Series labels ("Part 1 of 7", "Tomorrow: ...") so viewers follow.
- YouTube: playlists, end screens and chapters for longer videos.
- Invite duets and stitches ("Stitch this with your own result").
- Post at the same time every day.
- Weekly numbers check: MTG sends the retention graph (where viewers drop off) and the next scripts are tightened.
- Proof done honestly: invite viewers' own real results, with permission and no balances shown; never staged or invented proof.
- One clear next step in every video: link in bio to mtgtribe.com/altruva (ALTRUVA) or mtgtribe.com/group (the tribe), or the matching link for each project.

## Guardrails
Hope in learning and action, never guaranteed results or income promises. Respect, no pressure, guilt or fake urgency. Compliments only where true. "Write your own testimonial" means real results from their own action. No em dashes.
