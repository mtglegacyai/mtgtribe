JetUP and TAG Markets FAQ

The questions people ask before they start, answered plainly, with the risks left in.

Basics

What is JetUP?

JetUP is a community and partner program with training and AI tools. Its portal is jetup.ibportal.io and its hub is jet-up.ai. JetUP does not trade and does not hold your money.

What is TAG Markets?

TAG Markets is the broker. Its trading company, T.M. Financials Ltd, is licensed in Mauritius by the (licence GB21026474). Your trading account is opened there, in your own name.

How are JetUP and TAG Markets linked?

JetUP says on its own website that its trading accounts and transactions run through T.M. Financials Ltd, trading as TAG Markets. You create your TAG Markets account by tapping Register inside the JetUP portal (after the sync the button says Login to CopyX), so the two are linked.

What are CopyX and Sonic AI?

CopyX is TAG Markets' copy trading platform. is the strategy members connect: it trades gold (), and its track record is published on .

Money

How much do I need to start?

The training uses $155 in or USDC: $50 into Sonic AI, $100 left in your balance for the Dennis promo, and a few dollars spare. Keep a little extra on the exchange for the fee. Only use money you can afford to lose.

Which network should I use for the TAG Markets deposit?

In this order: USDT on BEP20 (BNB Smart Chain) first, USDT on TRC20 (Tron) second, and USDC on ERC20 (Ethereum) if you prefer it, which is the most expensive. Use the same network on both sides: the wrong network means lost funds.

Which exchange or wallet should I use?

VALR first, Binance second, or a Web3 wallet such as SafePal or MetaMask. The VALR and Binance buttons on this site are MTG's referral links.

How is profit split?

When the strategy makes a profit, 70% is yours and 30% is the , which pays the broker, the strategy provider and the partner program. There is no performance fee on a loss, and no management or join fee.

How do withdrawals work?

Profits can be withdrawn any time, through the broker. JetUP's one-pager says withdrawals take about 48 hours on average. With the Dennis promo, taking your own money out before 12 months ends the promo. Test a small withdrawal early.

After you join

How do I see my trades?

Install MetaTrader 5 (), add an account and search for TM Financials, the company behind TAG Markets. Log in with your account number and from CopyX, Follower Hub, Strategies (tap the eye icon). It is read only: you can watch every trade, not change anything. Sonic usually trades two or three times a day. Steps: mtgtribe.com/jetup/get-started/#mt5

Can I open another strategy later?

Yes. Deposit in TAG Markets, open CopyX, connect SONIC with the new amount and enter the two community codes again. Each one becomes its own account with the same rules. The Dennis Fast Start Promo is one time only, so a new strategy does not get another $100.

How do I withdraw my partner earnings?

In the JetUP portal open your Wallet, tap Withdraw and choose your TAG wallet or one of your MT5 accounts. To send money out, add your own crypto wallet address under Wallets first. Start with a small withdrawal.

Do I have to buy the BIX Card to link BIX?

No. Linking your BixPay wallet to the JetUP portal moves no money and costs nothing. It records your place in the team for BIX Card commissions. You can link it with a phone only: screenshot the QR code, then use Load from gallery in BixPay.

Risk

Can I lose money with JetUP and TAG Markets?

Yes. Forex, and copy trading are high risk, and with 24x the swings are bigger in both directions. You can lose your whole deposit. TAG Markets' own reminder says 88% of retail investor accounts lose money when trading CFDs.

What is Amplify 24x?

Amplify lets the account trade at 24 times its size: $50 trades like $1,200. That is , the size you trade with, not money you own. A drop of about 4.2% on the trading balance is the same size as the whole account.

When is an account closed (liquidated)?

TAG Markets' screen says: if the trader loses more than 10% of the starting balance, your account will be . With a , that limit becomes 5%.

Is JetUP a scam?

I can't answer that for you, and nobody honest can promise it either way. What you can do is check: your account is in your own name at a licensed broker, the strategy's results are public, the rules are written down, and some regulators have published warnings. The 5 checks show you where to look.

Promo and partners

What is the Dennis Fast Start Promo?

For new partners, once: your own $100 plus a $100 bonus open a Sonic AI account that trades as $4,800 with 24x Amplify. Profits can be withdrawn any time, the bonus is released after 12 months, and taking your own money out before then ends the promo. Rules can change.

What are the community token codes?

In part 5, after you type 50 in Investment, tap the arrow next to Community Token. Enter #9M and tap Verify, then 9MPromo and tap Submit. Both are case sensitive. With a community token, the loss limit becomes 5%.

Do I have to build a team?

No. You can use it as a client only. JetUP's partner plan pays partners who build a team, and JetUP's own founder says the big money comes from building a team. What a partner earns can be zero.
