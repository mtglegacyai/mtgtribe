The 5 checks before you try JetUP

If you've lost money in a trading platform, a copy trading app, or a program that promised you the world, this page is for you. You are not foolish for trying. The lesson is not to stop trying. The lesson is to verify first. So let's verify JetUP and TAG Markets, together.

Check 1 of 5

Who holds my money?

Your deposit goes into an MT5 account at TAG Markets, in your own name. MT5 means MetaTrader 5, the trading app many brokers use. JetUP is the community: it does not trade, and it does not take your money.

Check it yourself: don't take my word for it. Log in at portal.tagmarkets.com and see your own balance.

JetUP's own one-pager says: your money does not sit with JetUP, it sits in your own account at TAG Markets.

Check 2 of 5

Who regulates the broker?

Licence 1 · Mauritius

T.M. Financials Ltd, TAG Markets' trading company. Licensed by the FSC (Financial Services Commission, the Mauritius regulator), licence GB21026474.

Licence 2 · South Africa

TM Financials SA (Pty) Ltd, authorised by the FSCA (Financial Sector Conduct Authority, our regulator), FSP number 55237.

Read the fine print. TAG's own website says the South African company provides marketing services, and that trading accounts are held by the Mauritius company. So know which regulator covers your account.

Published regulator warnings. Regulators in Austria (FMA), Luxembourg (CSSF), Slovenia (ATVP), Russia (Central Bank) and Quebec, Canada (AMF) have published warnings that TAG Markets is not authorised in their countries. I'm telling you this because you deserve the whole picture.

Check it yourself

  • FSC Mauritius: search the register of licensees on fscmauritius.org for licence GB21026474.
  • FSCA: on fsca.co.za, go to Regulated Entities and search FSP 55237.
  • The global list of regulator warnings, IOSCO I-SCAN: search "TAG Markets" on iosco.org/i-scan.

Exposure: the size you trade with

$6,000

Not money you own

  • Bigger exposure means bigger swings, up and down. You can lose your whole deposit.
  • The loss limit: if the trader loses more than 10% of the starting balance, the account is liquidated (closed). With a community token, 5%.
  • The broker's insolvency cover is $1,000,000 in total, for all clients together, not per person. Insolvency means the broker itself going bust. Trading losses are not covered.

Check 5 of 5

Can I get out?

  • 💵 Profits can be withdrawn any time.
  • 📅 The promo bonus is released after 12 months.
  • ⛔ Taking your own money out before 12 months ends the promo.
  • 📲 Promo amounts change, so check the current rules in your dashboard before you start.

My advice? Start small, and do a small withdrawal early. Let the system prove itself to you.
