Create your JetUP account

Open the Partner link from your sponsor and fill in the sign-up form.

Watch MTG do part 1 (3:46)

  1. Open the Partner link from your sponsor. Every member has two links, Partner and Client: make sure it's the Partner link. If MTG invited you, tap any Join JetUP button on this guide. Invited by someone else? Use their Partner link.
  2. On the sign-up form, choose Individual.
  3. Type your first name and last name exactly as they are on your ID (no nickname, or your will not match), then your email and a password, using English letters only.
  4. Choose your country, then type your cell number without the first 0. The country code (for example +27) is already there.
  5. Your password: 8 to 12 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character (! @ $ % _ # ( )), with no spaces. Write it down somewhere safe: you use the same password for TAG Markets.
  6. Wait for the security check to show the green Success!, then tap Register Now. If it says the email is already registered, use another email, or log in with that one.
  7. Check your email and tap the verification link. Not in your inbox? Check spam or junk mail.

Success check: you see "Registration Successful. You can now log in." After you verify your email and log in, your profile shows Account Type: Partner.

Link JetUP to TAG Markets

One tap inside JetUP creates and links your TAG Markets account.

Watch MTG do part 2 (3:42)

  1. Log in at jetup.ibportal.io with the email and password you created in part 1.
  2. On your profile, scroll down to the banner and tap Register CopyX.
  3. Enter the same password you created on JetUP, read and tick the three boxes, then tap Sync Account. You'll see Syncing with Copy X..., then Sync Successful!.
  4. Tick "I understand that I need to review my email and validate my password..." and tap Close. The banner now says User Active and the button says Login to CopyX.

  6. Check your email for the TAG Markets email and tap Confirm email address. It can take a while: MTG's arrived after his first log in. Not in your inbox? Check spam or junk mail. You'll see "Verification successful". If the page does not move on, tap click here and log in again.

Verify your identity

Sumsub checks your ID and a selfie for TAG Markets.

Watch MTG do part 3 (3:37)

  1. On your TAG Markets dashboard (portal.tagmarkets.com), tap upload documents in "Please complete your KYC verification in order to make a deposit".
  2. Sumsub runs the identity check for TAG Markets. You opened it from TAG Markets, so tap Continue.
  3. Confirm your country of residence and tap Agree and continue.
  4. Under "Get verified faster with Sumsub ID", enter the same email you have used from the start and tap Start verification. Type the 6-digit code Sumsub emails you (not in your inbox? check spam or junk). Never share this code with anyone.
  5. Read the consent, tick the box and tap Create my account. You see "Sumsub ID account successfully created": tap Continue verification.
  6. Choose your issuing country and your document (MTG chose Identity Card). If it has two sides, tick My document is double-sided and tap Continue. Read the tips (a well lit room, no reflections, never edit the images) and tap Continue.
  7. When the browser asks to use the camera, tap Allow. Hold your ID inside the frame: it takes the photo by itself (move the phone back a little if it does not). Front, then back.
  8. Step 2 is a quick selfie: a well lit place, your face in the frame, no hat, glasses or mask. Hold still until you see All set.
  9. TAG Markets may also ask a few questions (your income, net worth, investment goal and whether you understand the risks): answer honestly. Read TAG Markets' short reminder (for example, the service is not for residents of the European Union), then continue.

"This site can't ask for your permission"? Close screen recorders and chat bubbles (for example WhatsApp), then tap Try again and Allow.

"Documents submitted on another profile"? You already have a TAG Markets profile with these documents. One person, one profile: do not open a second account. Ask your sponsor or TAG Markets support.

Fund your account

Send $155 from your exchange to your TAG Markets deposit address.

  1. Log in at portal.tagmarkets.com with the same email and password.
  2. Under My wallet balances, tap Deposit.
  3. Choose USD, tap Other and type 155.
  4. Tap Next, choose Crypto and tap Submit.
  5. Pick the coin and the network you are sending from (see the network ranking above). Copy the deposit address and the exact amount. The price is held for about 15 minutes, so keep moving.
  6. Open your exchange (the example is VALR). Choose withdraw, paste the address and choose the same network.
  7. Enter the amount and look at the fee. In the example it is 0.71 USDC on Ethereum: 155.00 + 0.71 = 155.71 USDC in total.
  8. For recipient details choose Individual, then Self-hosted Wallet. Check the address matches TAG Markets exactly, then tap CONFIRM WITHDRAWAL.
  9. Open the email VALR sends you and tap Authorize Transaction.

Match the network on both sides (for example BEP20 to BEP20), so your funds arrive safely.

Success check: back in TAG Markets you see "Payment found!", then "Payment successful." In the example it took about 3 minutes.

Connect Sonic AI

The part most people rush. Slow down here.

Code 1, then Verify#9MHashtag, nine, capital M
Code 2, then Submit9MPromoCapital M, capital P

Both codes are case sensitive. They come from MTG's team.

  1. Open the menu, tap CopyX, find SONIC (the first one in the list) and tap Details, then Connect. Don't pick any other trader.
  2. Read the terms: a 30% taken from profits only, no management fee, no join fee, a one month minimum, and an account.
  3. In Investment type 50. Only 50. The screen shows a $10 minimum, but 50 is the plan. Leave the other $100 in your balance for the promo.
  4. Read the account rules on this screen: the account closes if the trader's loss passes 10% of the starting balance. With a , that limit becomes 5%.
  5. Tap the arrow next to Community Token. Enter code 1, #9M, and tap Verify.
  6. A second field opens. Enter code 2, 9MPromo, tap Submit and wait for the tick.
  7. Tick both agreements. The second one says CopyX trades come from traders who are not part of TAG Markets. Then tap Copy Now.

Why the plan starts with $50: 24x Amplify makes every move bigger, in both directions, so a small start lets you learn how it moves.

Claim the Dennis Fast Start Promo

Your CU number unlocks the $100 promo bonus.

Watch the promo explained (2:18)

  1. In TAG Markets open the menu, tap Profile and write down your CU number.
  2. Go to jet-up.ai and tap the orange Dennis Fast Start Promo tile.
  3. Enter your name, email and CU number exactly as in TAG Markets, then tap Join the promo.
  4. You'll see Application received! Approval usually takes a few minutes and can take up to 24 hours. Wait for the confirmation email.
  5. When it arrives, open CopyX, then Follower Hub, then Strategies.
  6. You'll see two Sonic accounts: your $50 account ($50 × 24 = $1,200) and your $200 promo account ($200 × 24 = $4,800).

Know the promo rules: profits can be withdrawn any time. The $100 bonus is released after 12 months. Taking your own money out before then ends the promo. Rules can change, so check your dashboard. All the promo rules

After you're set up

Three things you'll want next

See every trade in MetaTrader 5

A read-only view of your own account. You can watch, not trade or withdraw.

  1. Install MetaTrader 5 () from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Tap New account (or add an account) and search for TM Financials, the company behind TAG Markets.
  3. In TAG Markets open CopyX, then Follower Hub, then Strategies, and tap the eye icon on one account.
  4. Copy the account number (your MT5 login) and the . Paste them into MT5 and tap Continue.
  5. Open History. Add your other account the same way.

Success check: your account opens in MT5. Sonic usually trades only two or three times a day, so a new account's history can be empty at first.

Open another strategy later

Your JetUP membership stays. Each new deposit can become its own Sonic account.

  1. In TAG Markets tap Deposit and fund your balance the same way as part 4.
  2. Open CopyX, find SONIC, tap Details, then Connect, and type the amount.
  3. Enter the two community codes again, exactly as in part 5. They set the Amplify, so don't skip them.

The Dennis Fast Start Promo is one time only. New strategies don't get another $100 bonus. Every account follows the same rules: 24x Amplify, the 10% loss limit (5% with a community token) and a one month minimum.

Withdraw or reinvest

Profits, and partner earnings if you share JetUP. See how the partner plan pays.

  1. In the JetUP portal (jetup.ibportal.io) open your Wallet to see partner earnings. Tap Withdraw and choose your TAG wallet or one of your MT5 accounts.
  2. To send money out, first add your own crypto wallet address under Wallets (for example VALR, Binance, SafePal or your BIX wallet), then choose it when you withdraw.
  3. In CopyX, Reinvest adds a strategy's profit back into that same strategy. It starts a new one month minimum for that account.

Start small and test a small withdrawal early. Fees, limits and timing are the broker's terms, so check them in your account.
