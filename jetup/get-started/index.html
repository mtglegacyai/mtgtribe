Create your JetUP account

Open the Partner link from your sponsor and fill in the sign-up form.

  1. Open the Partner link from your sponsor. Every member has two links, Partner and Client: make sure it's the Partner link.
  2. On the sign-up form, choose Individual.
  3. Type your first name, last name, email and a password, using English letters only.
  4. Choose your country, then type your cell number without the first 0. The country code (for example +27) is already there.
  5. Your password needs an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character, with no spaces.
  6. Read the two boxes before you tick them. The second one says leverage can amplify losses beyond your initial investment. Take that seriously.
  7. Pass the security check, then tap Register Now.
  8. Check your email and tap the verification link. Not in your inbox? Check spam or junk mail.

Success check: you see "Registration Successful. You can now log in."

Verify your identity

TAG Markets asks a few questions, then checks your ID.

  1. Answer TAG Markets' questions honestly: your income, your net worth, your investment goal, and whether you understand the risks.
  2. Read their reminder: not for residents of the European Union, you may have fewer protections than with a local provider, and 88% of retail investor accounts lose money when trading CFDs.
  3. The identity check is by Sumsub. Tap Continue, scan your ID, driver's licence or passport, and do the face scan. Use your phone for this.

CFD means contract for difference: you trade a price movement without owning the asset.

Success check: your KYC is accepted and you can log in to TAG Markets.

Part 4 of 6

Fund your account

Send $155 from your exchange to your TAG Markets deposit address.

  1. Log in at portal.tagmarkets.com with the same email and password.
  2. Under My wallet balances, tap Deposit.
  3. Choose USD, tap Other and type 155.
  4. Tap Next, choose Crypto and tap Submit.
  5. Pick the coin and the network you are sending from (see the network ranking above). Copy the deposit address and the exact amount. The price is held for about 15 minutes, so keep moving.
  6. Open your exchange (the example is VALR). Choose withdraw, paste the address and choose the same network.
  7. Enter the amount and look at the fee. In the example it is 0.71 USDC on Ethereum: 155.00 + 0.71 = 155.71 USDC in total.
  8. For recipient details choose Individual, then Self-hosted Wallet. Check the address matches TAG Markets exactly, then tap CONFIRM WITHDRAWAL.
  9. Open the email VALR sends you and tap Authorize Transaction.

Wrong network = lost funds. Check twice, on both sides.

Success check: back in TAG Markets you see "Payment found!", then "Payment successful." In the example it took about 3 minutes.

Connect Sonic AI

The part most people rush. Slow down here.

Code 1, then Verify#9MHashtag, nine, capital M
Code 2, then Submit9MPromoCapital M, capital P

Both codes are case sensitive. They come from MTG's team.

  1. Open the menu, tap CopyX, find SONIC and tap Details, then Connect.
  2. Read the terms: a 30% performance fee taken from profits only, no management fee, no join fee, a one month minimum, and an Amplify account.
  3. In Investment type 50. Only 50. Leave the other $100 in your balance for the promo.
  4. Read the screen most people skip: if the trader loses more than 10% of the starting balance, your account will be liquidated (closed). With a community token, that limit becomes 5%.
  5. Tap the arrow next to Community Token. Enter code 1, #9M, and tap Verify.
  6. A second field opens. Enter code 2, 9MPromo, tap Submit and wait for the tick.
  7. Tick both agreements. The second one says CopyX trades come from traders who are not part of TAG Markets. Then tap Copy Now.

Why it matters: with 24x Amplify, a drop of about 4.2% on the trading balance is the same size as your whole deposit.

Success check: you see "Success". Your $50 Sonic account is connected.

Claim the Dennis Fast Start Promo

Your CU number unlocks the $100 promo bonus.

  1. In TAG Markets open the menu, tap Profile and write down your CU number.
  2. Go to jet-up.ai and tap the orange Dennis Fast Start Promo tile.
  3. Enter your name, email and CU number exactly as in TAG Markets, then tap Join the promo.
  4. You'll see Application received! Wait for the confirmation email.
  5. When it arrives, open CopyX, then Follower Hub, then Strategies.
  6. You'll see two Sonic accounts: your $50 account ($50 × 24 = $1,200) and your $200 promo account ($200 × 24 = $4,800).

Know the promo rules: profits can be withdrawn any time. The $100 bonus is released after 12 months. Taking your own money out before then ends the promo. Rules can change, so check your dashboard. All the promo rules

Success check: both Sonic accounts show under Strategies.
