Every word, explained

The JetUP and TAG Markets glossary

The words you will meet in this guide, in plain English. Tap any underlined word on the other pages to see its meaning right there.

2FA

Two-factor authentication: a second code, usually from an app on your phone, that you enter when you log in.

See it explained

Amplify (24x)

TAG Markets' setting that lets an account trade at 24 times its size. Gains and losses both get 24 times bigger.

See it explained

CFD

Contract for difference: you trade a price movement without owning the thing itself.

See it explained

Community token

A code from your sponsor's team, entered when you connect Sonic AI. The training says that with it, the loss limit becomes 5%.

See it explained

CopyX

TAG Markets' copy trading platform. It copies a strategy's trades into your account.

See it explained

CU number

Your TAG Markets client number, shown under Profile. You need it to claim the JetUP promo.

See it explained

Exposure

The size you trade with, not money you own. $250 with 24x Amplify trades like $6,000.

See it explained

FSC

Financial Services Commission of Mauritius: the regulator that licenses T.M. Financials Ltd, the company behind TAG Markets.

See it explained

FSCA

Financial Sector Conduct Authority: South Africa's financial regulator.

See it explained

Investor password

A read-only password for your MT5 account. It lets you watch every trade in MetaTrader 5, but not trade, change or withdraw anything.

See it explained

KYC

Know Your Customer: proving who you are, usually with an ID document and sometimes a proof of address.

See it explained

Leverage

Trading with more than your own money, which makes gains and losses bigger. Amplify is TAG Markets' version of it.

See it explained

Liquidated

Closed. The account is closed when the loss limit is reached.

See it explained

Lot

A standard trade size. JetUP's lot commission is counted per lot traded in your structure.

See it explained

MT5

MetaTrader 5: the trading app many brokers use. Your TAG Markets trading account is an MT5 account.

See it explained

Myfxbook

A website that publishes trading results, so you can look at a strategy's record before you deposit.

See it explained

Network

The blockchain route a coin travels on, for example Tron (TRC20). The sending side and the receiving side must use the same network.

See it explained

Partner link

The sign-up link for partners. Every member has a Partner link and a Client link: use the Partner link to join as a partner.

See it explained

Performance fee

The 30% of strategy profit that pays the broker, the strategy provider and the partner program. It is only taken from profits.

See it explained

Recovery phrase

The secret words that restore a self-custody wallet. Write them down offline and never share them with anyone.

See it explained

Self-custody

You hold the keys to your own crypto wallet, so only you can move the funds.

See it explained

Sonic AI

The strategy members connect on CopyX. It trades gold (XAU/USD), and its record is published on Myfxbook.

See it explained

USDT and USDC

Digital dollars (stablecoins) that you send from an exchange to fund your TAG Markets account.

See it explained

XAU/USD

Gold priced in US dollars: XAU means gold, USD means US dollar.

See it explained
