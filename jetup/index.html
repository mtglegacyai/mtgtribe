Who does what

Five names. One simple picture.

Most confusion starts when these get mixed up. Tap each one and watch where it sits, from you to the strategy.

Who does what

You, the account holder

You open the account, you fund it and you decide. Your money sits in an account in your own name at TAG Markets, and you can log in and see your own balance any time.

An everyday picture

Think of a minibus taxi 🚐

You don't drive. You choose a driver, take your seat, and the driver does the driving. Copy trading works the same way.

  • 🧑‍✈️Sonic AI is the driver. It does the trading.
  • 💺Your seat is booked in your name. That's your TAG Markets account.
  • 🪧CopyX is the route board that puts you in that driver's taxi.
  • 👥JetUP is the association. It trains and organises the passengers.

Where the picture stops: a taxi gets you there. A strategy can lose money, and a good last trip never promises the next one.

Straight answers

JetUP and TAG Markets questions

What is JetUP, and how is it linked to TAG Markets?

JetUP is a community and partner program with training and AI tools. TAG Markets is the broker. JetUP says on its own website that its trading accounts and transactions run through T.M. Financials Ltd, trading as TAG Markets. Your money sits in your own account at TAG Markets, not with JetUP.

Is JetUP with TAG Markets safe?

Nobody can promise that. What you can do is check: who holds your money, who regulates the broker, whether the strategy's results are public, what can go wrong and how to get out. MTG's 5 checks walk you through each one, with the sources, so you decide with your eyes open.

How much do I need to start?

The sign-up training uses $155 in or USDC: $50 goes into the Sonic AI strategy, $100 stays in your balance for the Dennis Fast Start Promo, and a few dollars stay spare. Only use money you can afford to lose.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Copy trading and CFDs are high risk, and with 24x Amplify the swings are bigger in both directions. You can lose your whole deposit. TAG Markets' own reminder says 88% of retail investor accounts lose money when trading CFDs.

Is this official JetUP or TAG Markets material?

No. This is an independent guide by MTG. It quotes JetUP and TAG Markets where it uses their figures, and it shows you where to check them yourself.
