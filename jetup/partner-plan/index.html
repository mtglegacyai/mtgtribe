JetUP's partner program

The partner plan, in plain English

JetUP pays partners four ways, all tied to trading at TAG Markets. Here they are, with JetUP's own figures, and with the rules most people miss. Read this as information, not a promise: what a partner earns can be zero.

  • JetUP's figures, as of 4 Oct 2026
  • Check current terms in your partner account

Before any number: these are JetUP's own figures. JetUP's plan says there is no fixed, daily or guaranteed return and no guaranteed income, so results depend on the market and on the work you put in. New to the trading side? See how 24x and the 70/30 split work in How it works.

Who qualifies

The rule most people miss

  • JetUP's plan says everyone needs at least $250 of active trading to qualify as a partner.
  • A $250 own deposit opens 3 levels. With 1 to 3 direct partners it is still 3 levels. From the 4th direct partner, each new direct partner opens 1 more level. 10 direct partners open all 10.
  • The sign-up training uses $155 ($150 of your own money in the strategy and the promo). That's a client start. If you want to earn as a partner, check the current qualification in your partner account first.

Income 1 · Lot Commission

Up to $8.00 per lot, 10 levels deep

A fixed amount for every (a standard trade size) traded in your structure. Counted from real traded volume, not from deposits. Paid from broker margin.

Level 1: $2.00. Levels 2 to 4: $1.00 each. Levels 5 to 10: $0.50 each. Total $8.00 per lot.

Income 2 · Profit Share

1.5% on each of 10 levels

When the strategy makes a profit, partners receive 1.5% of that strategy profit on each of 10 levels of their structure. It is paid out of the 30% share, never from client deposits. Levels open the same way as above.

Income 3 · Infinity Bonus

A leadership bonus, no depth limit

An extra percentage on top of the Profit Share, from level 1 with unlimited depth, set by the new team volume each calendar month (not your accumulated total).

  • The 50% rule: at most half of your qualifying volume can come from one line, so Infinity needs at least two strong lines.
  • The difference rule: if a partner in your line reaches their own Infinity level, you earn the difference between your rates on their structure.

Income 4 · Global Pool

For very large structures

  • Pool 1: 1% of profit plus $1 per lot, from $300,000 of fresh capital a month.
  • Pool 2: 1% of profit plus $1.50 per lot, from $1,000,000 of fresh capital a month.
  • Qualification is confirmed every month, the 50% rule applies, and it is paid from broker margin.

Income 5 · BIX Card

Commissions from card sales and spending

Half of the fees BIX collects (the 3% spending fee and the card purchases) goes into the plan: 15% on level 1, 10% on level 2, 2.5% on each of levels 3 to 10, and 5% to a leadership pool, as shown in a JetUP partner presentation (November 2026).

It only counts once your BixPay wallet is linked to the JetUP portal. How to link it, even from a phone.

The honest summary

JetUP's own founder says the big money comes from building a team, not from the trading percentage. So if you share this, share it the right way: teach first, be honest about how it works, and never promise anyone an income. Say "potential business partners", and treat the people who join as business partners.
