How to read it

Exposure is the size you trade with. With the training example, $250 × 24 = $6,000. A 1% move on $6,000 is $60, which is 40% of your own $150.

The move that equals all your own money is $150 ÷ $6,000 = 2.5%. The move that equals the whole balance is 100% ÷ 24 = 4.17%, which is why the training says a drop of about 4.2% is the same size as your whole deposit.
