Questions

Good to know

How do I get the Zoom link?

The Zoom link is shared in the MTG TRIBE WhatsApp group. Join the group, or message MTG directly on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab) and ask him for the link.

What time is it where I live?

Every time on this page is South African time (SAST, UTC+2, no daylight saving). The time zone table shows Nigeria, the United Kingdom, Canada and US East and Australia, plus a Your time column worked out from your own device.

Can I join from my phone?

Yes. Zoom works on phones, tablets and computers. Install the Zoom app on your phone, then tap the link when it is shared in the group.

Who can attend the training night?

Sunday 8pm is the back office training night, for members. If you are not sure whether it is for you, ask in the MTG TRIBE WhatsApp group.
