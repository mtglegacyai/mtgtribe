# The ClickBaitPays message (MTG, 22 November 2026)

The one angle behind every ClickBaitPays page, script and video MTG makes: tutorials, explainers, reels, shorts, ads,
promo videos, captions and posts. Read this before writing any ClickBaitPays copy. It wins over the official brochure.

> "If you have a link for it, you can promote it on ClickBaitPays, get traffic, while you make money with ClickBaitPays at
> the same time, increasing your streams of income. ... This is my biggest selling point." (MTG)

## The promise in one line

**Got a link? Get it seen.** ClickBaitPays puts any program with a link in front of real people who open it, every click
day, and pays you in USDT to view their ads while it happens.

It is a **traffic source**, not an investment. Say it that way, every time.

## Who it is for (name them; this is what people really promote)

BellverCards, Bitcoin Wealth, JetUP, XFUSION, Solar Group, Aureus Alliance, stokvel and smart contract platforms, MLM
programs, affiliate programs, digital products, their own business. "If it has a link, it belongs on ClickBaitPays."

Not "your shop, a YouTube video, your WhatsApp group": MTG called that weak (22 Nov 2026). Lead with the programs people are
building today.

## Why they need it (the pain, in their words)

- Every business needs eyes. Every team needs sign-ups. Every affiliate needs clicks.
- Team builders and MLM leaders: a team grows one yes at a time; more eyes on your link means more chances at a yes.
- Stokvel and smart contract platforms: they run on new members, and ClickBaitPays members already fund accounts in crypto.
- Affiliate marketers: no clicks, no commissions.
- Digital product sellers: a great product nobody sees is a secret, not a business.
- Any business: a shop on a street nobody walks down sells nothing.

## Analogies that land (use them, say where they stop only when it helps)

- A billboard on the busiest street in town, except every passer-by is paid to stop, open it and look for 15 seconds.
- Fishing with a net, not a single line.
- The busy street versus the empty street.
- A community noticeboard where members are paid to read each other's posters.

## Why the visitors act

- They already use crypto: they fund accounts in USDT, so wallets, deposits and sign-up links are everyday stuff.
- They are already looking: opening ads is part of their day; a 15-second view with full attention, not a thumb flick.
- A page that explains with video lets the ones it speaks to say yes on their own (this is why MTG's guides have videos).

## Three streams of income, one account

1. **Your program grows**: the ad brings real people to BellverCards, Bitcoin Wealth, JetUP or whatever you promote.
2. **Your clicks pay**: USDT per view, from $0.48 (Level 1) to $13.50 (Level 7), for 3 to 20 ads a day.
3. **Your referrals pay**: 10% of the campaigns your referrals buy.
