## 3. The script standard (every ClickBaitPays video)

The locked formula still runs the show (greeting, In this video, steps, recap, next video, fair play, signature ending).
MTG's 22 Nov 2026 layer on top of it:

1. **Greet like family.** "Hi, my name is MTG, Your Digital Architect. Thank you for taking the time to watch this video."
   Then one warm line that welcomes and congratulates them for the step they are on ("Welcome, and congratulations: you
   are about to place your very first ad"). In South Africa we greet before we teach.
2. **Sit next to them.** Speak to one person at the table: "Let's do this together." "See this button, top left? Tap it."
   "Now look here." Exact on-screen labels, one action at a time, and the success check: "If you see Click day complete,
   you got it right."
3. **Teach one word.** Every video makes the term its step touches crystal clear, in the website's own words (click day,
   activation, hold period, Available Balance, CPC 90%, Preview, Login Ad, Pay It Forward, Security PIN, USDT).
4. **One everyday picture.** An analogy people live with (a taxi rank, a queue, a shop on a busy street, a billboard, a
   community noticeboard, a season ticket, a harvest), and where it stops when that matters.
5. **Open their eyes.** One moment that shows what is possible, tied to the step: Got a link? Get it seen; BellverCards,
   Bitcoin Wealth, JetUP and XFUSION are links too; the three streams in one account; MTG's own BellverCards story; your own
   copy of this website. Never an income promise.
6. **The smart nuggets.** 2 or 3 in a short video, up to 5 in a long one: the tip that saves trouble ("Preview first: test
   the door before you invite guests"), the reason behind a rule, the thing most people miss. The viewer should think "this
   guy knows what he is talking about".
7. **Show them we see it their way.** Name their worry and answer it calmly ("New to crypto? That's fine, this is how I
   did it too").
8. **Next video.** Close with what the next video in the lineup does, and why it comes next.
9. **Short, never thin.** Cut every wait and detour; never cut a step, a success check or a nugget. Targets: tutorials 3
   to 7 minutes, explainers 60 to 120 seconds.

**The opening and the close (MTG, 22 Nov 2026), every video:**

- **After the greeting, the outline:** what this video covers, in 2 or 3 short lines while the list fills in on screen.
- **At the end, the recap:** "In this video, you learned ..." with the same list ticking off, "we showed you this and that".
- **Close with a bang:** a strong call to action in the style of MTG's best closers on bellveraffiliates.com (read that
  site's closing lines before writing; reuse their energy, not other programs' claims), then thank them and make them feel
  great for watching to the very end, then the way forward: the next video, and after the last video in the series, the
  next action (Join, Get This Website, My Income Streams).
- **One closing quote for the whole ClickBaitPays series**, the same words at the end of every ClickBaitPays video (as
  Leo's quote closes the Bitcoin Wealth videos). Never vary it. The quote: see `SERIES_QUOTE` below (MTG picks it).

SERIES_QUOTE: not chosen yet (proposed 22 Nov 2026: "One ad. The right person. The right time.", from MTG's own
BellverCards story). Write MTG's choice here, then use it word for word.

**Look and feel:** zoom in on the action and out for context, arrows and rings on what to look at, emoji pops on key words,
smooth transitions, captions with the spoken word lit, a step progress bar, the official ClickBaitPays emblem where MTG's
photo would be, Orbitron headings, every amount with its $ sign ($300 USDT, +$30 activation). Sounds: only the signature
four (whoosh, tick, pop, click). Privacy: username, balances and other members' ads hidden in every frame.

**Congruent with the website, always.** Words, figures and steps come from the site (`cbp/_build/build.py`: PROGRAMS,
STORY, LEVELS, FAQ, TUT) and `MESSAGE.md`. A video never contradicts the page it plays on; if a video shows a step the page
lacks, the page gets it too.

**Approval and credits:** one script at a time for MTG's approval; ask before spending HeyGen credits; preview before
anything goes live.
