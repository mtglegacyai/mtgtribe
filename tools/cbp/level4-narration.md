# Tutorial E (Level 4 walkthrough): MTG's narration

ClickBaitPays walkthrough: log in, buy a Level 4 campaign, activate Level 4 earning, and click my daily ads.
Recorded by MTG on 21 November 2026. Raw file: silent desktop recording, 17:37, 1906x978, 25 fps (Google Drive id
1uLKnoaa5Q_gZQy5y7m65y8FBj0TYS4k7). The video starts once the username and password are typed: ignore anything before
Log In. MTG's own text, word for word, except that his account balances are replaced with [balance] (this repo is

---

## 1. Logging in

I type my username and password and tap Log In. As always, a login ad appears first. I tap Open Ad. The ad opens in a
new tab; I don't have to stay on it, so I close it and wait for the 30-second countdown. When the countdown ends, I tap
Go To Dashboard.

## 2. My dashboard before I start

My available balance is [balance]. I didn't have this before: all my earlier campaigns have run their course and paid
out. That is why my dashboard shows 0 active campaigns, 0.00 in my wallet and 0 clicks today: I have no ads running at
the moment.

So today I buy a new campaign and activate a new level.

## 3. Buying a Level 4 campaign

On the left of my dashboard I tap Buy Ad Campaigns. The levels are listed. I already have Levels 1, 2 and 3 activated,
so today I go to Level 4.

Level 4 works like the other levels: its price is $300, with unlimited views and 12 click days. I tap Buy This Ad
Campaign. The page confirms that my balance of [balance] is sufficient.

Next, my ad needs a title. A title can be at most 30 characters long. I tap in the title box and pick a punchy title
from the list I saved before: "Become a Bellver affiliate". I could also type one in.

Then the link: https:// is already filled in, so I add bellveraffiliates.com. I tap Preview to make sure the page opens.
I know mine works, but when you do this yourself, always tap Preview first, so you know that what you are promoting
really opens.

I tap Confirm Pages. A window asks me to confirm ("You are selecting 2 pages, level 4 ad campaigns. Please confirm this
before moving forward."), and I tap Yes, I Am Sure.

The next screen says: "Ad campaign level 4 purchased. Your ad is now live."

## 4. Activating Level 4 earning (once only)

Because this is my first Level 4 campaign ever, the same screen asks whether I want to earn from clicking Level 4 ads,
which needs the earning activation fee. I tap Yes, Activate Now.

The checkout page, Level 4 Earning Activation, shows what each click pays at this level, that I get 5 ads to click each
day, and a one-time fee of $30. The fee unlocks earning for all my Level 4 campaigns, so the next time I buy a Level 4
campaign I won't pay it again.

Each level, from Level 1 to Level 7, is activated once, and the activation never expires. These activation fees also
support the company's sustainability.

After the $300 campaign my balance is [balance], which is sufficient for the $30. I tap Confirm Activation, then Yes, I
Am Sure in the confirmation window. The screen says: "Level 4 earning activation purchased. You can now earn from
clicking level 4 ads. This activation never expires."

## 5. Clicking my 5 ads for the day

Now I go to Click Ads And Earn. It shows 1 active campaign (I had 0 when I started) and 5 clicks remaining today. The
ads are listed with their titles, so I can see what each one is about before I open it.

For each ad: I tap View And Earn, then Open Ad. The advertiser's page opens in a new tab. I can browse it, or close it
and come back. The countdown (15 seconds) runs, then the confirm button becomes active and I tap it to claim the click.
Then I move on to the next ad.

These are real people promoting real products and services. If something interests me, I can browse their site and buy
from them. We are not viewing ads just for the sake of it.

If one ad's page is blocked by my security software, I don't need to open it. I can still wait for the countdown and
confirm the click. If an ad looks interesting, I tap Save Ad to keep it and watch it later, as often as I like. On the
last ad today, "We make websites that sell", I tapped Save Ad and it now says Saved.

I repeat this until all 5 ads are done for the day.

## 6. Saved ads

Under Saved Ads I find every ad I saved, with its link, so I can go back to it at any time. To take an ad off the list,
I tap Remove. After removing one, I have 7 saved ads left.

## 7. My dashboard after clicking

My available balance is now [balance], and I have 1 active campaign.

How this level works for me: each click at Level 4 pays $6.48, and I get 5 clicks a day. That is $32.40 a day from my
$300 campaign, for 12 click days, about $388.80 in total, as long as I view my ads every day. After the 12 days there is
a 7-day cool-off period before that money can be withdrawn or used to buy a bigger campaign.

## 8. What I can do with my available balance

My available balance is mine to use:
- Withdraw it: request a withdrawal to my external wallet.
- Pay it forward: use some of it to help someone who wants to join but only has fiat money, for example a team member.
  There is a separate tutorial video for paying it forward.
- Buy another campaign: smaller ones cost $13, $77 or $150.

My own plan: I am working my way up to Level 7. So I am not spending the [balance] now. When I have about another $100,
I will have $300 again and buy a second Level 4 campaign. Then I keep building until I can move up to Level 5, and so on.

I hope this walkthrough has been helpful.
