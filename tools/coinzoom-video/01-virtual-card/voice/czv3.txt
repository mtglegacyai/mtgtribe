In this video, I'll show you how to find your virtual Coin Zoom Visa debit card in the app. Three quick steps: tap Spend, tap Reveal, and double tap to confirm.

Once your virtual crypto debit card is activated, it lives right inside your Coin Zoom app.

Step one. Log in, and tap the Spend tab at the bottom of the screen. This opens your card screen.

Step two. Make sure Virtual Card is selected, then tap Reveal.

Step three. The app asks you to confirm it's really you. Double tap to confirm.

And there it is! Your card number, cardholder name, expiry date and C V V. Tap Copy to copy your card number.

Keep these details private, and only enter them when you pay online.
