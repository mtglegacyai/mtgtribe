In this video, I'll show you how to load cash into your Coin Zoom account. Four quick steps: tap Deposit, tap Coin Zoom Cash, pick a store, and show your barcode.

Coin Zoom Cash lets you load cash at participating stores across the U S, like Speedway, Stripes and Seven Eleven.

Step one. In your Wallet, tap Deposit.

Step two. Tap Coin Zoom Cash.

Step three. Use the map to pick a store near you. It only shows stores that take Coin Zoom Cash.

Step four. At the store, pull up your barcode, and ask the cashier to scan it.

Your barcode has a timer, and the screen shows the fee and the limits, so read them first.

The cashier loads your cash and gives you a receipt. You're done!

Let's recap. You tapped Deposit, chose Coin Zoom Cash, picked a store, and the cashier scanned your barcode.
