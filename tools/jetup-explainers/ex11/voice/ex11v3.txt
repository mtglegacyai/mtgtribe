Ten levels is not the limit. Meet the Infinity Bonus: no depth limit at all.

It's a leadership bonus on top of the Profit Share, from level one, all the way down.

Think of a bicycle with gears. Your gear is your rate, set by your team's new volume each month: from one and a half percent at fifty thousand dollars, to seven and a half at forty million.

And the trip meter resets every month: only that month's new volume counts, paid once a month.

Rule one, the fifty percent rule: your strongest line counts for at most half. A bike needs two strong wheels.

Rule two, the difference rule: if a partner in your line reaches their own rate, you earn the difference on their structure. Three percent minus their one and a half leaves you one and a half.

So that's Infinity: your gear on your whole team, two strong wheels, and the difference rule.
