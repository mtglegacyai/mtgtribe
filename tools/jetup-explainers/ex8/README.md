## The picture

A fruit tree in your own backyard: spoken "a fruit tree", drawn as a mango tree, built one piece at a time. New to the
series (not the taxi, the market stall, the glass floors or the bus rank). Each part keeps its colour and label in every
scene.

- The backyard, behind a gate signed "TAG Markets · the broker", with your name plate on the gate: your account at TAG
  Markets, in your own name.
- The tree is your own money. The fruit is your profit, when the strategy makes one (two or three mangoes, never a
  laden tree). Ripe fruit you can pick whenever you like: profits can be withdrawn any time.
- A gift branch tied onto your own tree: the Dennis Fast Start Promo's $100 bonus, which comes with your own $100 (new
  partners, once). It is released after 12 months, so let your tree stand 12 months to keep the promo.
- Your basket outside the gate: your own crypto wallet (for example your BIX wallet). The path through the gate:
  withdrawals go through the broker, to your wallet address, on the same network on both sides, about 48 hours on
  average (JetUP's one-pager); fees and limits are the broker's terms. Shown as the real thing (a labelled
  illustration), not forced into the tree, and naming no wallet inside TAG Markets until MTG confirms how Sonic AI
  profits come out.
- One small fruit first: the small mango from the hook is the first fruit picked (nothing is picked before it): test a
  small withdrawal early and let the system prove itself. A key in your own name plate: log in yourself and see your
  own balance.
