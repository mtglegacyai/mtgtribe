## The picture

A cooking class: spoken "a cooking class", drawn as a community-hall cooking class (rows of tables, enamel pots on
two-plate stoves, the chef in a plain apron and a white hat), built one piece at a time. New to the series (not the
taxi, the market stall, the glass floors, the bus rank or the fruit tree). Each part keeps its colour and label in every
scene: the chef gold, your pot, its name plate and the CopyX line lime, the glass lid clear.

- The chef at the front is Sonic AI, the strategy: he makes the moves, and his hat badge reads "Sonic AI · the
  strategy". Each stir is a trade.
- Your own pot on your own table, with your name plate, is your own account: an MT5 account at TAG Markets, in your
  name. The ingredients in it are your money, which stays in your own account. Every other table has its own pot and its
  own name plate: each member copies into their own account, and there is no shared pot.
- In a class you copy each step yourself, with your own spoon. The twist: you don't even lift the spoon. The lime line
  labelled "CopyX" from the chef's pot into yours (and into every other pot) is CopyX, TAG Markets' copy trading
  platform: the strategy's trades are copied into your account automatically. The spoon rests on its rest for the rest
  of the video: you don't pick the trades.
- Your three choices, three numbered slots beside your table: the strategy (which chef: SONIC, first in the CopyX list),
  how much to put in (an unmarked jug: the training uses 50, the screen's minimum is $10) and when to stop (your own
  raised hand: your call, in the site's own words, with no small line and no stop steps).
- The glass lid on your pot is the MetaTrader 5 app: with your investor password you can watch every trade in your own
  account, read only (watch, not trade or withdraw).
- The proof is the real thing, MTG's own CopyX screens, not forced into the picture: SONIC first in the list with "Check
  the record yourself: Myfxbook", the fee on one enlarged row in the screen's own label (Performance Fee 30%, taken from
  profits only), and the eye icon where the investor password lives.

Never shown (storyboard rule), so the picture suggests no trade sizes, no timing, no exit rule and no results: no
amounts or markings in any pot or jug, no stir drawn bigger or smaller than another, no two pots side by side to
compare, no clock or timer, no "live" or "same time", nothing for "when to stop" that switches, turns, flips or counts
days (no switch, no knob, no calendar), no finished dish, tasting or score, no volume or heat knob, no flames or boiling
over, no nonstop stirring (the chef stirs once, then rests), and no MT5 trade rows, balances or performance figures
anywhere, cover included. The voice says only "cooking class", "chef", "pot", "spoon" and "glass lid", so the drawing
can change for free.
