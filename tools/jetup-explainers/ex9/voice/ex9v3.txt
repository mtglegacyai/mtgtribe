Copy trading. What gets copied, and what stays yours?

Think of a cooking class. The chef cooks at the front, and you copy each step, in your own pot.

And the twist? You don't even lift the spoon.

Your pot is your own account.

So what gets copied? The strategy's trades, into your account, automatically. You don't pick the trades.

And what stays yours? Your own account, and your choices. You pick the strategy, how much to put in, and when to stop.

Want to see it for yourself? Your pot has a glass lid: the MetaTrader five app. With your investor password, you can watch every trade, read-only.

So that's copying. The trades get copied. Your money stays in your own account, you make your three choices, and you can watch every trade.
