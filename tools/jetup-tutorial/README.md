## Part 1: create your JetUP account (notes from MTG's narration, 7 Nov 2026)

1. Open the Partner link (MTG's site button, or your sponsor's link) in the phone's browser. The screen says **Sign up**:
   "Please complete the form using English alphabet characters only."
2. **Individual** is selected by default. Register as an individual, with your **real names** (as on your ID).
3. Enter your email.
4. Create a strong password that is easy for you to remember and hard to guess. **At most 12 characters** (MTG hit the
   limit and removed a character). Show the password rules exactly as they appear in the video.
5. Choose your country, then your cell number **without the first 0** (the country code is already there). Double-check it.
6. Wait for the Cloudflare check to say **Success**, then tap **Register Now**.
7. Tip card (from an error MTG hit, do not show the error itself): if the email address is already registered,
   registration fails ("email address already exists"). Use a different email, or log in with the one you already have.
8. You see **Creating account**, then **Check your email**: "We've sent a verification link to your email address. Please
   click the link to activate your account." Tap **Return to login**.
9. Optional tip: on your own phone you can let the browser save the password.
10. Open your email: a message from **IB Portal**, "Verify your IB Portal account". Scroll down and tap **Verify Account**.
11. The browser opens: **Email verified**, "Your email has been successfully verified. You can now log in." Tap
    **Continue to log in**.
12. Log in with the email and password you registered with. Success check: you are inside the JetUP portal.

Differences from the current site, to apply when the video is in: the 12 character password limit; the Cloudflare
"Success" check; the "email already exists" tip; the email sender "IB Portal" and the **Verify Account** button; the
"Email verified" screen.
