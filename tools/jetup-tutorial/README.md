## Part 1: create your JetUP account (notes from MTG's narration, 7 Nov 2026)

1. Open the Partner link (MTG's site button, or your sponsor's link) in the phone's browser. The screen says **Sign up**:
   "Please complete the form using English alphabet characters only."
2. **Individual** is selected by default. Register as an individual, with your **real names** (as on your ID).
3. Enter your email.
4. Create a strong password that is easy for you to remember and hard to guess. **At most 12 characters** (MTG hit the
   limit and removed a character). Show the password rules exactly as they appear in the video.
5. Choose your country, then your cell number **without the first 0** (the country code is already there). Double-check it.
6. Wait for the Cloudflare check to say **Success**, then tap **Register Now**.
7. Tip card (from an error MTG hit, do not show the error itself): if the email address is already registered,
   registration fails ("email address already exists"). Use a different email, or log in with the one you already have.
8. You see **Creating account**, then **Check your email**: "We've sent a verification link to your email address. Please
   click the link to activate your account." Tap **Return to login**.
9. Optional tip: on your own phone you can let the browser save the password.
10. Open your email: a message from **IB Portal**, "Verify your IB Portal account". Scroll down and tap **Verify Account**.
11. The browser opens: **Email verified**, "Your email has been successfully verified. You can now log in." Tap
    **Continue to log in**.
12. Log in with the email and password you registered with. Success check: you are inside the JetUP portal.

Differences from the current site, to apply when the video is in: the 12 character password limit; the Cloudflare
"Success" check; the "email already exists" tip; the email sender "IB Portal" and the **Verify Account** button; the
"Email verified" screen.

## Part 2: link JetUP to TAG Markets (notes from MTG's narration, 7 Nov 2026)

1. Log in at **jetup.ibportal.io** with the email and password you registered with in part 1.
2. Scroll down to the bottom of the page to the **CopyX banner**: "To access all platform features, please register your
   TAG Markets account exclusively using this button. Registering through IB Portal ensures proper linking and
   synchronization between both accounts." Never register on CopyX or TAG Markets separately.
3. On MTG's new account the button says **Register CopyX** (older screenshots say "Login to CopyX": same step).
4. The screen asks for your password: "At this moment we are going to sync your account. Please continue." Enter the
   **same password you created on JetUP** in part 1.
5. Tick the boxes, then tap **Sync Account**. You see **Syncing with CopyX**, "please wait while we verify...", then
   **Sync successful**: "Your account has been linked with TAG Markets. You will receive an email to validate your password
   and complete activation. If you do not see it, please check your spam or junk."
6. Tick "I understand that I need to verify my email and validate my password to finish the TAG Markets..." and close.
7. Success check: the banner says **User Active**. Tap it to go to TAG Markets (portal.tagmarkets.com): "Checking your
   browser", then the TAG Markets log in.
8. Log in with the **same email and the exact same password** (up to 12 characters, as created on JetUP). Then "Checking",
   "Verify you are human", "Please wait", and you are on the TAG Markets dashboard (portal.tagmarkets.com). It says
   "Complete your KYC verification in order to make a deposit", with an **Upload document** link (that is part 3).
9. Now the TAG Markets email arrives (in MTG's recording it came only after this first TAG Markets log in). Open it and
   tap **Confirm email address**. Cloudflare checks, then **Verification successful**: "Your email address has been
   successfully validated. You will now be redirected to the client portal. If you are not redirected within a few
   seconds, please click here to log in."
10. If nothing happens, tap **click here**, and log in again with the same email and password (on your own phone you can
    let the browser remember the password). Success check: you are back on the TAG Markets dashboard with your email
    confirmed. **Confirm the email before KYC.** End of part 2. Part 3: verify your identity (KYC).

## Part 3: verify your identity, KYC (notes from MTG's narration, 7 Nov 2026)

1. On the TAG Markets dashboard (**portal.tagmarkets.com**, show the address bar): "Please complete your KYC verification in
   order to make a deposit". Tap **upload documents**.
2. The Sumsub screen loads: "Verification for TAG Markets Ltd. You are about to submit sensitive data to TAG Markets Ltd. If
   you received this link from a suspicious source, please close this page and notify us immediately." You opened it from
   TAG Markets, so tap **Continue**.
3. Confirm your country, then **Agree and continue**.
4. "Let's get you verified": provide an identity document, then a liveness check. "Get verified faster with Sumsub ID":
   enter **the same email you have used from the start** (stay consistent), then **Start verification**.
5. "Verify your email to use Sumsub ID": enter the code Sumsub sends to that email (check your inbox).
6. "Save your data with Sumsub ID": give consent and create the account: "Sumsub ID account successfully created". Tap
   **Continue verification**.
7. Choose the document (MTG: **Identity card**). If the card has two sides, tick **double-sided**. Continue.
8. "Get ready to upload your ID card": read the tips, Continue, and **Allow** the camera.
9. The camera captures automatically when the card fits the frame (move the phone back a little). Front, then back.
   "Step 1 of 2" submitted.
10. Step 2, "Get your camera ready": the selfie and liveness check (face and background in view), "Hold still", "All set".

Tip cards from what happened to MTG (do not show the struggle):
- **"This site can't ask for your permission. Close any bubbles or overlays from other apps"?** Close screen recorders and
  chat bubbles (for example WhatsApp), then tap Allow again. MTG's screen recorder caused it, so the camera steps are not
  in his recording: show them with his screenshots and clear cards.
- **"We couldn't verify your profile because your documents have been submitted on another profile"**: you already have a
  verified TAG Markets profile. One person, one verified profile. Contact your sponsor or TAG Markets support before going
  further. (MTG got this message: he is checking which TAG Markets account he already has.)
