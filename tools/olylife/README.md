### 13 Nov 2026: how-to video, extracted

A South African OlyLife distributor at home, 5 minutes, vertical. The audio is noisy, so this combines two transcripts
with the frames; only what the video shows or clearly says is listed.

1. **Clean the plates first, every time.** Spray 70 to 75% alcohol hand sanitiser (any brand) on a cloth and wipe both
   plates. Wipe again after each person.
2. **The power lead.** It is a standard detachable mains lead: South African 3-pin plug on one end, a kettle-type
   (IEC) connector on the other, the same kind as many desktop computers and appliances. If it goes missing, that
   type of lead replaces it.
3. **The back of the unit** (see `ref/back-panel.jpg`): power socket, a USB port, a rocker on/off switch, and a round
   button (possibly a fuse holder; check the manual). Plug in and switch on at the back.
4. **Set up.** Machine on the floor, chair behind it. Sit, put both bare feet flat on the plates.
5. **The display** (see `ref/feet-and-display.jpg`) shows minutes and level. Read from the seat, "30 01" means
   30 minutes at level 1. From the front it looks upside down ("10 0E").
6. **Levels.** First time: level 1 (she says "01 or 02"). She goes up to level 2 during the demo. The following week,
   level 2 or 3.
7. **30 minutes, then the machine rests 20 minutes** before the next person. She repeats this several times.
8. **Drink water** after the session.
9. She says she uses it twice a day, 6 hours apart (the instructions allow up to twice a day, 4 to 6 hours apart).
10. Closing: "So easy to use this machine." Her lines "you heal naturally" and "your body will react" are claims we do
    not repeat.

**Useful for building:** a short "Your first session, step by step" explainer or tutorial (clean, plug in, switch on,
sit, level 1, 30 minutes, water, clean, rest 20 minutes), a hygiene promise for the booking page ("plates cleaned with
alcohol before and after every person"), and the capacity planning (one session about every 50 minutes per machine).

## MTG's own visit (14 Nov 2026)

MTG went to a centre that runs sessions, saw and touched the machines and did a 30-minute session (display "30 01":
30 minutes, intensity level 1, the lowest, where first-timers start). Confirmed in person: R350 per 30-minute session
for people without their own machine; a glass of water before and after; sessions at least 6 hours apart. He felt no
difference after the first session and said so plainly; he met people on their 3rd and 4th session. He is doing 4
sessions over 4 weeks and logging them in `my-sessions.md`, for an honest testimony either way.

OlyLife's official YouTube channel (OlyLife International) celebrated its 3rd anniversary about 10 months before
14 Nov 2026 (seen by MTG), which fits the company's 2022 registration in Malaysia: about 4 years old.
