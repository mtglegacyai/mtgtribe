## How to use it (as shown and instructed)

1. Put the unit on the floor in front of a chair. Plug the mains lead into the back, switch on at the back.
2. Clean both plates with 70 to 75% alcohol hand sanitiser on a cloth before each person.
3. Sit upright and comfortably. Both bare feet on the plates, soles in good contact, feet slightly apart (not
   touching), knees slightly apart, hands apart and relaxed on the thighs.
4. Session is 30 minutes (the unit stops itself). Start at level 1 or 2. The display, read from the seat, shows
   minutes left and the level (for example "30 01": 30 minutes, level 1).
5. Drink water before and after. Wait about an hour before a shower.
6. Clean the plates again. Let the machine rest about 20 minutes before the next person.

**How often:** week 1, once a day; from week 2, up to twice a day, 4 to 6 hours apart. 30 minutes maximum per session.

**During a session (OlyLife-branded "Correct Sitting Position" card shared by SA distributors, Nov 2026):**
- Do not use a cell phone or other electronic device; keep them away from you.
- Do not cross the legs or arms. Do not let the legs or knees touch each other.
- Do not hold or use any device that emits electromagnetic frequencies.
- When finished, switch the unit off before getting up.
- The card's own line: "Not a substitute for medical treatment. Check with your healthcare professional if you have an
  implanted electronic device or medical concerns."

**Capacity of one machine:** 30-minute session plus 20-minute rest, so about one session every 50 minutes, at most
about 9 in an 8-hour day.

## Who must not use it, or must ask a doctor first

Pacemaker or other implanted electronics, metal implants, pregnancy, children (OlyLife says under 18; some say under 14),
heart disease, a bleeding tendency (including during menstruation, uterine fibroids), fever, impaired consciousness.
**Reduced feeling in the feet (diabetic feet, neuropathy):** the manual cautions this group because they may not feel a
burn starting. Nobody stops prescribed medicine because of the machine.

**Recorded injury report:** FDA MAUDE MW5186559 (event 24 Oct 2025, filed by a nurse): "Multiple patients seen with
burns, wounds and injuries utilizing olylife tera p90", hospitalisation. Unverified report, not a finding.
