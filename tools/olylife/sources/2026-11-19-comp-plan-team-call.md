## The basics he describes

- **Binary tree:** every distributor has a left (A) and right (B) leg. One rule: your first person goes on the left;
  after that you choose. People your upline brings in can be placed under you ("spillover").
- **No autoship:** no monthly order needed to keep a rank or volume.
- **Daily payouts:** a sale today shows in the back office tomorrow.
- **Point value (PV):** every product carries PV, lower than its price. Examples given: Shaker Massager $1,000 =
  750 PV; the "perfect wellness package" (P90+ $1,500 plus G1 goggles $500) = $2,000 = 1,500 PV.
- **Entry purchases mentioned:** $1,000, $2,000 or $6,000 of product. The size of your first purchase sets several
  caps and percentages below. (A critical review found tiers from $100 to $6,000: P1 Starter $100, P2 Builder $500,
  P3 Leader $1,000, P4 Supervisor $2,000, P5 Pioneer $6,000. Check which applies.)

## The six ways it pays (as he explains them)

| # | Bonus | How it works (his words, summarised) | Caps and conditions he mentions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Direct sale bonus** | 10% of the PV of every product you sell. $2,000 package = 1,500 PV = $150; Shaker Massager = $75; a $6,000 purchase = $450 | The one you control: demos, sessions, people trying the machine |
| 2 | **Team bonus (upline bonus)** | 5% of whatever the person directly above you in the tree earns that day (and the person below you earns 5% of yours) | Total capped at 3 times your first purchase: $1,000 in = $3,000; $2,000 = $6,000; $6,000 = $18,000 |
| 3 | **Tier bonus** | Each time a new tier has its first person on the left and on the right, the tier "closes" and pays. Counts people placed by anyone in your tree | $375 to $2,250 per tier ($750 in his $2,000 examples). Volume never flushes; unlimited levels |
| 4 | **Global pool** | Reaching **M1** (10,000 PV on the left and 10,000 PV on the right) earns 1 share; M2 = 2 shares, M3 = 4 shares. OlyLife puts 5% of a year's revenue into the pool and splits it by shares at year end | Shares kept for life if you sell at least one $1,000 product a year. He says OlyLife does "80 plus million dollars a month" in global sales (unverified). He reached M1 in 10 days |
| 5 | **Group sales bonus** | A percentage of the PV on your lesser (weaker) leg each day: 11% after a $1,000 purchase, 12% after $2,000 | Capped per day ($2,000 a day at the $2,000 level). Example: weaker leg 15,000 PV x 12% = $1,800 |
| 6 | **Coaching bonus** | 10% of what each person you personally brought in earns, plus 5% of what their people earn, down to level 6 | No cap, he says. Example: your 8 directs earn $14,000 in a day = $1,400 to you |

His worked example: after finding 6 people (with others placed around him), 7 tiers closed, about $6,100 earned and
M1 reached, all assuming everyone buys the $2,000 package.

## Questions to answer before MTG decides (honest checklist)

1. **Get the official plan and the policies document**, not slides from a team call. Check every percentage, cap
   and the entry packages above.
2. **Ask for an income disclosure statement:** what do most distributors actually earn in a year? The call shows
   best-case examples (everyone buys $2,000, legs fill up, people below recruit).
3. **Where does the money come from?** Bonuses 2, 3, 5 and 6 grow with recruiting and placing people, and the caps
   grow with how much you buy at the start. South African law (the Consumer Protection Act) forbids schemes where
   income comes mainly from recruiting rather than from selling products to real customers. Find out what share of
   OlyLife's sales goes to customers who are not distributors.
4. **What does MTG have to buy to start, and what does a bigger buy-in unlock?** The plan ties caps and percentages
   to the first purchase, so there is pressure to start big. Decide the amount on its own merits (for MTG: one
   machine for his sessions), not to raise a cap.
5. **Returns and refunds** on a first purchase, and what happens to commissions if a customer returns a machine.
6. **The "80 to 90 million dollars a month" figure:** ask for the source.
