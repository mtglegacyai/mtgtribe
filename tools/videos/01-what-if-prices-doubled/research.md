# Research brief: What If Prices Doubled Every Day? (7 November 2026)

Job: reach (primary) and tribe growth (secondary). Stage: discover. Platforms: YouTube 16:9 (about 5 min) plus 3
vertical Shorts for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels and WhatsApp status.

## Demand

YouTube searches run on 7 November 2026 with `yt_research.py` (sorted by views): "zimbabwe hyperinflation",
"100 trillion dollar note", "hyperinflation explained", "what if prices doubled every day".

- The topic is evergreen: the top Zimbabwe and 100 trillion videos have 2.1M to 9.8M views each.
- The top ones are old: the two biggest are 7 years old. There is no fresh, high-quality 2026 version.
- The "doubling every day" format is hot right now: "How Many Days Can I Double $1?" 28.7M views in about a year, and a
  run of "doubled my budget every day" videos from the last 3 to 8 months at 1.2M to 3.6M views each (370K to 714K
  views per month).
- "Money is an idea" videos are strong too: "Once You Understand MONEY, You'll Stop Saving It" 3.5M in 6 months.

## Thumbnail patterns (sheet of the top 12 checked)

One hero object (the banknote, a loaf of bread, a $1 bill), one huge number, 2 to 4 words, high contrast, often a face.
The bread with a giant price is already proven for this exact topic.

## The gap

- Made for the world, not for Africa: no South African angle, no rand, no "this happened next door".
- Old: the biggest versions are 7 years old.
- Several frame it as mockery or politics ("worst economy ever", land and farmers); real families get lost.
- None turn it into a lesson people can use today about the money they hold, including crypto, honestly.
- No conversation built in: the comments are left to chance.

## Our "better than" plan

1. Ride the hot "doubling every day" format, flipped into a true story: prices, not a budget, doubled.
2. A fresh 2026 version with verified facts and sources on screen.
3. Africa first: rand prices, "right next door to South Africa", and the Johannesburg agency that printed posters on
   real banknotes (Cannes Lions Grand Prix, 2009), a beat the top videos do not lead with.
4. A visual for every number: the doubling price tag, the shrinking pay envelope, all 14 zeros counted on screen.
5. Respect, not mockery: the families who lived through it.
6. An honest lesson for today: diversify, know what stands behind every note, coin or token; stablecoin and Bitcoin
   risks named, no pitch.
7. Built-in conversation: guess the zeros (paid off later), "what would you buy first?", "money is what?", a
   specific share prompt.
8. MTG's warmth and a real next step: the MTG TRIBE.

## Decisions

- Length: about 5 minutes (645-word body plus greeting and outro). The proven winners run 8 to 12 minutes; 5 minutes
  keeps it tight for a growing channel, and the Shorts carry the reach.
- Titles: A "What If Prices Doubled Every Day? (It Really Happened)", B "The Country Where Prices Doubled Every Day",
  C "Money Became Cheaper Than Paper".
- Thumbnail A: a 3D loaf with a giant price tag "R10,737,418,240" and a "DAY 30" chip, MTG's circle photo on the right.
  Thumbnail B: an illustrated note (not a copy of the real design) with "14 ZEROS" and "IT HAPPENED".
- Hook: the rand bread thought experiment, then the reveal.
- Prompts: guess the zeros; what would you buy first; finish the sentence "money is..."; send it to the friend who
  keeps all their savings in one place.
- Main address: mtgtribe.com/group (said and shown at the bridge), plus the pinned comment and description.

## Facts and sources

- Prices doubling about every 24.7 hours at the peak, November 2008 (Hanke and Kwok; Hanke and Bostrom):
  https://sites.krieger.jhu.edu/iae/files/2018/07/Zimbabwe-Hyperinflates-Again-Hanke-Bostrom-.pdf and
  https://www.cato.org/publications/commentary/inflation-inferno-roared-away-until-mid-november-2008
- The Z$100 trillion note, January 2009: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwean_one_hundred_trillion_dollar_note
- Zimbabwe dollar abandoned April 2009, US dollar and rand used: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwean_dollar_(1980%E2%80%932009)
- The Trillion Dollar Campaign (TBWA Hunt Lascaris, Johannesburg, March 2009; "It's Cheaper To Print This On Money Than
  Paper"; Cannes Lions 2009 Outdoor Grand Prix): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Trillion_Dollar_Campaign
- The maths: R10 doubled 30 times is R10,737,418,240; halving every day for 7 days leaves under 1%.
- The "printing money faster than goods" cause is broadly accepted; the full list of causes is debated (said so in the
  script).
