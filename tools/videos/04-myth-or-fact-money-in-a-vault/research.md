## The gap

Millions of views for long explainers on "where is my money really", but the one-minute version is 10 years old and
abstract. No short, friendly, South African version that also says what protects your money today (deposit insurance).

## How ours will be better

1. A myth everyone believes, busted in the first line ("Myth or fact ... Myth.").
2. One picture for the main number: 100 squares, 97 are bank deposits, 3 are notes and coins.
3. The balance shown as what it is: a promise to pay you.
4. A queue at the bank shows a bank run without jargon.
5. Calm, not scary: banks are regulated, and South Africa insures deposits up to R100,000 per person, per bank (CODI).
6. Ties back to the tribe's big idea from videos 01 and 02: money is a promise plus trust.

## Facts (verified 9 November 2026)

- Bank of England, Quarterly Bulletin 2014 Q1, "Money in the modern economy: an introduction": "in the United Kingdom,
  97% of the money held by the public is in the form of deposits with banks, rather than currency."
  https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/quarterly-bulletin/2014/q1/money-in-the-modern-economy-an-introduction (PDF read).
- South African Reserve Bank, Corporation for Deposit Insurance (CODI): from 1 April 2024, qualifying deposits are
  protected up to R100,000 per depositor, per bank (including interest), automatically, at every registered bank.
  https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home1/what-we-do/Deposit-insurance and the SARB launch statement
  https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/south-african-reserve-bank-launches-corporation-deposit-insurance-25-apr-2024
- A bank run is when many depositors withdraw at once and a bank cannot pay everyone in cash at the same time.
