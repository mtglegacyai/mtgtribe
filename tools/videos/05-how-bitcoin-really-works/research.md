## Facts (verified 9 November 2026)

- bitcoin.org FAQ (https://bitcoin.org/en/faq): "only 21 million bitcoins will ever be created"; "New blocks are added
  to the blockchain approximately every 10 minutes on average"; "The number of new bitcoins created each year is
  automatically halved over time"; "Bitcoin is controlled by all Bitcoin users around the world."
- The Block, 9 March 2026 (https://www.theblock.co/post/392660/bitcoin-mined-supply-hits-20-million-btc): the 20
  millionth bitcoin was mined (block 939,999), more than 95.2% of the 21 million cap; the reward is 3.125 BTC since the
  20 April 2024 halving (from 6.25); halvings every 210,000 blocks, roughly every four years; the last bitcoin around
  2140.
- Reward history used on screen (follows from the halving every 210,000 blocks, starting at 50): 50 (2009), 25 (2012),
  12.5 (2016), 6.25 (2020), 3.125 (2024).
- "Thousands of computers": Bitcoin's network of nodes run by its users worldwide (bitcoin.org: controlled by all
  users); we say "thousands", not a precise count, because counts vary by method.
- Keys, not coins: a wallet stores the private keys that sign transactions; the coins are entries on the shared record.
  Lose the keys (and the recovery phrase) and nobody can restore them (video 03 covered the 12 words).
