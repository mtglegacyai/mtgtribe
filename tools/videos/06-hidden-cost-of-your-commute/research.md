## The gap

The winners are long (5 to 32 minutes), American or Filipino, and negative: commuting hurts your health, your job
keeps you poor. Nobody gives the one-minute, South African, hopeful version: here is the number, your job is fine, and
here is what twenty minutes of that trip can become.

## How ours will be better

1. One person, first second: "If your trip to work takes an hour ...".
2. A big number in the first five seconds that people can check themselves: 60 working days a year.
3. The maths shown line by line, so nobody has to trust us.
4. A reframe instead of a complaint: the hidden cost is time, not the fare.
5. Respect: "Your job matters. Keep it." No shaming, no "escape the 9 to 5".
6. A small, doable step: 20 minutes a day is 80 hours a year, two full working weeks of learning.
7. A question everyone can answer (how long is your trip?) and a share prompt to a real person (the friend who rides
   with you), then the tribe.

## Facts and maths (checked 11 November 2026)

- Example in the video, stated as an example: 1 hour there + 1 hour back = 2 hours a day; × 5 days × 48 working weeks
  = 480 hours a year; ÷ 8-hour working days = 60 working days. 48 weeks is shown on screen as the assumption.
- 20 minutes × 5 days × 48 weeks = 4,800 minutes = 80 hours = 10 working days = two full working weeks.
- South Africa, Stats SA National Household Travel Survey 2020: workers took 63 minutes to get to work by taxi (50
  minutes in 2013) and 107 minutes by train (74 in 2013). Source read: Daily Maverick, 5 March 2021,
  https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2021-03-04-millions-of-people-walk-all-the-way-to-their-destination-in-south-africa-says-survey/
  ; BusinessTech, 5 March 2021 (same survey: most taxi commuters took 30 to 60 minutes; over eight in ten train
  commuters more than 60), https://businesstech.co.za/news/motoring/473242/how-much-it-costs-south-africans-to-get-to-work/
  . The Stats SA release itself (P0320) could not be opened from here (blocked), so the on-screen line names the survey
  and the year and says "about an hour".

## Limits

The survey is from 2020, the newest national one found. The hour each way is an example, not a claim about every
viewer. No income figures: "a second income" is named as something to build next to the job, with time and work.
