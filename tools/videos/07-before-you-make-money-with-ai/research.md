## The gap

The hottest field in his niche right now (9.6M views in one month for a single AI money video), but the winners are
8 to 33 minutes long and sell "laziest" and "easiest". Nobody gives a beginner the honest one-minute version: what to
do first, with one skill they can use the same day and no promise attached.

## How ours will be better

1. A big true number in the first three seconds: 900 million people use ChatGPT every week.
2. Speaks to the viewer's wish ("before you try to make money with AI") without promising anything.
3. An open loop: "Number two is the skill that sets you apart."
4. Three numbered steps anyone can follow: a problem before a tool, a clear brief, check the work.
5. One practical formula to save: WHO it's for, WHAT you want, HOW it should sound, shown with a real small example.
6. Honest: AI can sound sure and be wrong; "you are the editor"; start small, no promises.
7. A question (which one first?), a share prompt (a friend who is curious about AI), then the tribe.
