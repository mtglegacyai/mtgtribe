# Research brief: Why Most People Never Start a Second Income (and the Small First Step) (15 November 2026)

Daily video 10. Hook format: Why Most People ... (rotation slot 10, the last of the first round). Topic family:
full-time employees and multiple streams of income (09 was gold, 08 Bitcoin history, 07 AI). Job: reach + comments +
tribe growth. Tone: warm, encouraging and respectful of the job; inform, never frighten; no income promises.

## Who it talks to

One full-time employee who has thought about a second income for years and never started. The video never tells them
to quit, never shames them, and gives one step small enough to do today.

## The gap

The winners are long lists of ideas with income figures in the title. Almost nobody talks to the person who already
has a list of ideas and still has not started, and almost nobody does it in one minute without an income claim. The
video for fully employed people (845K views) shows the audience is there.

## How ours will be better

1. It names the real problem: not a lack of ideas, but a first step that looks too big.
2. A true number in the first two seconds (about one in four adults has a second income), with its source on screen.
3. Real survey answers for what stops people, shown with their shares and their source.
4. One step anyone with a job can take today: 20 minutes a day on one skill, with the arithmetic shown.
5. Honest: "It won't make you rich overnight", and "No income promises" on screen.
6. A question that tells MTG what his viewers need next (money, ideas or time), a share prompt to one specific person
   ("the colleague who keeps saying one day"), a save prompt, then the tribe.

## Facts (verified 15 November 2026)

- "Only about one in four adults has a second income": Bankrate's Side Hustle Survey, released 9 July 2025: 27% of
  U.S. adults say they have a side hustle (36% in 2024, 39% in 2023). 2,616 U.S. adults, YouGov panel, 2 to 4 June
  2025. On screen: "U.S. adults with a side hustle: 27% (Bankrate survey, 2025)". Source:
  https://www.bankrate.com/press-releases/fewer-americans-have-a-side-hustle-in-2025/
- What stops people: a GOBankingRates survey (reported 27 July 2023) found 42% of Americans have considered starting
  a business; among the reasons they have not: "Not enough money" 43.58%, "Don't know what type of business to start"
  15.76%, "Not enough time" 12.94%. On screen as 44%, 16% and 13%, with "GOBankingRates survey on starting a business,
  2023". Source read: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nearly-half-americans-considered-starting-140058309.html
- The arithmetic: 20 minutes x 365 days = 7,300 minutes = 121.7 hours, said as "about one hundred and twenty hours a
  year"; at 8 hours a day that is 15.2 days, said as "fifteen full working days".

## Limits

Both surveys are from the United States; the screen says so for the first, and names the source for the second. The
second survey asked about starting a business, not specifically a second income, and its sample size is not given in
the report read; two of its answers are left out because they are not hurdles ("No interest" 15.66%, "Already own a
business" 12.06%). "The first step looks too big" is MTG's reading of the three answers, presented as a way to look
at them, not as a survey finding. The video promises no income and names no product or program.
