Daily video 12. Hook format: What If (rotation slot 2). Topic family: stocks (the first stock video; 11 was smart
contracts, 10 employees, 09 gold). Job: reach + education + tribe growth. Tone: curious and calm; education only, no
tips, no predictions, no recommendation; the risk named once, gently.

## Who it talks to

One person who uses big brands every day and has never thought of owning a piece of them. The video explains three
words (share, dividend, index fund) and gives one first step that costs nothing: look up the companies behind your
brands.

## The gap

Stock basics pull tens of millions of views, but the winners are long lectures and many promise income ("How I make
$68K/month"). The "what if you owned the brand" angle exists only as tiny videos about past returns of one stock.
Nobody explains share, dividend and index fund in one minute through things the viewer already buys, without a
return or a pick.

## How ours will be better

1. A question about something the viewer did today (bought a drink) in the first second, and a big true number (2.2
   billion drinks a day) in the first seven.
2. Three words, one picture each: a slice of the pie (share), coins flowing to owners (dividend), hundreds of tiny
   companies falling into one basket (index fund).
3. One real, sourced example (64 years of dividend increases), shown as an example, not a recommendation.
4. Honest: "Prices go up and down, and there are no promises."
5. One free first step anyone can take today, written on a notepad, with a save prompt.
6. A fun share prompt ("the friend who drinks the most Coke") and the tribe.

## Facts (verified 17 November 2026)

- "Its drinks are served two point two billion times a day": The Coca-Cola Company, "The Coca-Cola system": "This
  efficient system delivers 2.2 billion refreshing moments daily." Source read:
  https://www.coca-colacompany.com/about-us/coca-cola-system
- "Coca-Cola has raised its dividend sixty-four years in a row": company press release of 19 February 2026, "Board of
  Directors of The Coca-Cola Company Elects New Officer and Approves 64th Consecutive Annual Dividend Increase"
  (quarterly dividend from 51 to 53 cents). Source:
  https://investors.coca-colacompany.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1152
- "The S and P five hundred puts five hundred big American companies in one basket": the S&P 500 index of S&P Dow
  Jones Indices tracks about 500 leading U.S. companies (about 80% of the available U.S. market value; at times 503
  securities, because some companies have two share classes). An index fund that follows it holds those companies.
- A share is a unit of ownership in a company; a dividend is a part of the profits some companies pay to their
  shareholders. General definitions.

## Limits

Coca-Cola is named as a well-known example only; the screen says "An example, not a recommendation." No logo and no
brand colours are shown (the bottle is an original gold drawing). No price, no return and no forecast; the screen says
"Education, not advice." No platform, broker or product is named.
