One person who keeps hearing "copy trading" and wants to know, simply, what it is and what to check before trying it.
Also the person who was burned before: MTG's five checks page speaks to them directly.

MTG's JetUP and TAG Markets guide on mtgtribe.com (live on the main branch: `jetup/`) already explains copy trading

## Demand

Copy trading and trading-for-beginners videos are a large, proven category (video 12's searches: "How To Start Trading
Stocks As A Complete Beginner", TradingLab, 5.5M views; Mark Tilbury's investing videos, 6 to 7 million each). Most
winners are long and many show profits or promise income. The gap: a one-minute, calm explanation of how copy trading
works plus the questions to ask before you start, with no screenshots of returns.

## How ours will be better

1. A big, sourced number in the first five seconds ($9.5 trillion a day in currency markets).
2. MTG's own everyday picture (the minibus taxi), animated: you don't drive, you pick a driver, take your seat.
3. How the copy works, shown with round example numbers (a $10,000 strategy and a $1,000 account: the same trade, one
   tenth the size), clearly labelled as an example.
4. Five questions that protect the viewer, ticked one by one, with a save prompt.
5. The risk named once, gently, with what to do (start small, learn first).
6. A clear next step: the full guide to the five checks, free in the tribe.
