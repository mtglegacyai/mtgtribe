## The gap

"Zero investment" and "with $0" videos pull millions of views, but they are long, they lean on income figures, and
many promise "easy" or "lazy" money. Nobody answers the money myth in one minute with plain fact checks and an honest
verdict ("mostly myth", with the real costs named).

## How ours will be better

1. A question the viewer answers in their head in the first second (myth or fact?), and a real number (44%) in five.
2. Three fact checks, each one picture: what you need, tools with free plans (price tags flip to $0), a start-up
   receipt with the real costs.
3. An honest verdict stamped on the claim: mostly myth; money helps you go faster, skills and time get you started.
4. No income claims; "Results take patience. No promises."
5. A save prompt ("Save this checklist") and a share prompt with a reason ("the friend who says I'll start when I have
   money").
6. A callback to video 10 (the same survey), so the series builds on itself.

## Facts (verified 19 November 2026)

- "Forty-four percent said not enough money is what stopped them from starting": GOBankingRates survey (reported 27
  July 2023): among Americans who have not started a business, "Not enough money" 43.58%. Read for video 10:
  https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nearly-half-americans-considered-starting-140058309.html
- "A website name is about twenty dollars a year or less": a .com name costs the registrar $10.97 a year from
  1 November 2026 (Verisign's wholesale price, raised from $10.26) plus a $0.20 ICANN fee, so at least about $11.17;
  retail prices in October 2026 ranged from $10.46 (Cloudflare, at cost) to $18.68 (Namecheap), so after the 7% rise
  about $11 to $20. On screen: "about $11 to $20 a year, depending on where you buy it (Nov 2026)". Sources:
  https://webhosting.today/2026/05/21/verisign-starts-a-four-year-com-price-cycle-the-first-7-hits-in-november/ and
  https://costgoat.com/compare/domains/com
- "Many of the best tools have free plans": general and checkable (design apps, AI assistants and portfolio sites with
  free plans); no brand is named, and the screen adds "Free plans have limits; paid plans add more."

## Limits

The survey is about starting a business in the U.S.; the screen names it. Domain prices change by registrar and
promotion; the screen gives the range and the month. No income figure, no program, no product, no "easy".
