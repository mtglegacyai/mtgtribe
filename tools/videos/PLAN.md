**Two rules that shape every topic (from MTG's other sessions, 8 and 9 November 2026, read on 11 November):**
- **Inform, never frighten.** "Take it easy on the warnings and on the losses and negativity ... create videos that are
  going to convert these people into action takers." Calm, positive, encouraging; no loss statistics, no piled-up
  warnings, no scare hooks. Honesty stays: no income promises, risks named once, gently.
- **Never make a video that works against what MTG promotes.** His JetUP and TAG Markets guide covers CopyX copy trading
  with the Sonic AI gold strategy, so no "AI trading is a scam" or "copy trading is dangerous" videos. Scam-awareness
  videos stay on clear-cut cases (someone asking for your 12 words, fake support, "guaranteed profits").

Each video still gets its own research before the script, and the order can change when research shows a hotter
topic. Facts marked "to verify" are checked during that video's research, never assumed.
